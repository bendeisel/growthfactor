So the pile does not rebuild

Five rules

  1. Ideas become issues, not branches.This is the whole fix. Right now an idea turns into a fully implemented PR, which costs a full session. An issue costs thirty seconds and loses nothing. Twenty-two open PRs is twenty-two sessions of work that never landed.
  2. One open PR per rock at a time.When there are two, the second one is a queue pretending to be parallelism.
  3. Merge or close inside seven days.A PR older than a week is a decision you already made and never recorded.
  4. No client build starts before the sitemap is signed off.NMMA's 84 duplicate URLs are what building without one looks like three years later.
  5. Friday closes, it does not start.Merge, close, append the shipped-log row, stop. At 100 sites that log is the only thing standing between a factory and a template mill.
