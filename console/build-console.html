The pipeline

    

      Three ways a build starts, one way it gets reviewed, one way it goes live.
    


    

      

        Step 1Build
        One of the three build routines. Produces one artifact holding
        every page.
      

      

        Step 2Preview
        Split to real pages, uploaded noindex to
        preview.growth-factor.ai/<slug>.
      

      

        Step 3Approve
        Client opens the link and says yes. Nothing ships before this.
      

      

        Step 4Ship
        Split for production with canonical and sitemap, deployed to
        their domain.
      

    


    

      The preview URL survives launch. Production is a separate
      Hostinger website, so shipping a site touches nothing under
      previews/. Both URLs stay on the build's row, which is how
      two builds are told apart without opening either.
