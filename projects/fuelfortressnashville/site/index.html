Questions

Frequently asked.

Is the gym open 24 hours?

Yes. Members have access 24 hours a day using the QR code issued at signup. Staffed hours are Monday to Friday 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Saturday 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM and Sunday 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

How much is membership?

$84.99 a month on the annual rate, $104.99 month to month, or $25 for a day pass. Military, first responder and student discounts are available with valid ID.

What is included?

The weightlifting floor, the sauna, kickboxing and 24 hour access at all four Fuel Fortress locations across Kentucky and Tennessee.

What costs extra?

Personal training and ready-made meals. That is the whole list.

Do you run classes or programs?

No. There is no class timetable and no program to enrol in. The one exception is a couple of recreational kickboxing sessions a week, which come with your membership.

Can I sign up in the middle of the night?

Yes. Sign up online at any hour, get your QR code immediately, and scan in the same night.

Where are you?

412 Davidson Street, Nashville, TN 37213, between downtown and East Nashville.
