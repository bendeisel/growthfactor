The Gym

What's actually
in the building.

No programs to sign up for and no class schedule to work around. A 24 hour gym where you get the free weights, the recovery room and the door code. The rest is yours.

The Weightlifting Floor

Racks, competition platforms, free weights and a dumbbell run that goes heavy enough to matter, with plate-loaded machines along the far wall. Whether you are chasing a powerlifting total or building for bodybuilding, it is laid out so you can superset without crossing the building or waiting out somebody's phone break.

The Sauna

Cleaned daily and open to every member at no extra charge. Sit in it after a heavy session, sleep better that night, and come back Wednesday without your legs arguing about it.

Kickboxing

Teryn, one of the owners and our GM, is a former fighter who runs a couple of kickboxing sessions a week for anyone who wants to hit something. It comes with your membership at no extra cost, and it is recreational rather than a program.

Recovery

Plenty of gyms in Nashville list a sauna on the amenities page, then hide it behind a broken door in the corner of the locker room.
