Recovery

A sauna you'll actually want to sit in.

Plenty of gyms in Nashville have a sauna listed on the amenities page and then hide it behind a broken door in the corner of the locker room. Ours gets wiped down every single day, it runs hot when you get there, and it is included with every membership — no upcharge, no booking window, no add-on fee.

Recovery is standard here, not a luxury add-on.

Add-Ons

Available, but not
part of the membership.

Two things we offer on top of your membership. Both cost extra, both are optional, and neither one is bundled into your monthly rate — we'd rather say that plainly than surprise you at the desk.

  • 01

    Personal Training

    We have trainers in the building and you can hire one. One-on-one coaching built around your goals, your body, and your timeline, priced separately from membership. Ask at the front desk and we'll match you with someone.

  • 02

    Ready-Made Meals

    Ready-made meals available in-store, built on Fuel Nutrition (2018) — because results start with what you eat. Grab them on your way out. Sold separately.

  • 03

    Kickboxing

    Taryn — one of the owners and our GM — is a former fighter who runs a couple of kickboxing sessions a week for anyone who wants to hit something. It's free for members and it's recreational, not a program.
