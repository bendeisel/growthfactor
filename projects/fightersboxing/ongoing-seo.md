## The strategy in one line

Fighters cannot out-spend a franchise, and does not need to. It has two things
no competitor in Nashville can copy: **an Olympic-level coaching lineage** and
**a real nonprofit doing real community work in schools**. Both are link
magnets. Neither is currently being used.

## 1. The backlink engine: the Boxing Resource Center

This is the highest-leverage ongoing work, and it is why FBG can outrank gyms
with bigger ad budgets.

BRC is a registered nonprofit (EIN 75-3055338, GuideStar profile live) with
named, genuinely newsworthy programs:

- **Hit the Books After-School**: boxing and literacy for middle-school students
- **Hit the Books Boxing League**: a 9-week league across middle Tennessee
  schools, run with school and certified boxing coaches
- **Let Them Box**: advocacy for women's Olympic boxing, part of the story of
  women's boxing reaching the 2012 Olympics
- **Float/Sting**: a non-contact program built on Ali's gold-medal style

Schools, foundations, local press and sports bodies link to things like this.
They do not link to a gym's pricing page.

### Who to pitch, in priority order

| Target | Angle | Link type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Middle schools in the league | Their club page lists the program and partner gym | `.k12` / school domain, very high trust |
| Metro Nashville Public Schools | After-school partner listing | Institutional |
| Nashville Scene, Tennessean, Nashville Banner, WPLN | Halbert already won Best Boxing Coach 2025. The league is the follow-up story | Editorial, highest value |
| USA Boxing, Tennessee LBC | Sanctioned club, events, coach certifications | Authoritative sports body |
| Local foundations and grant makers | Grantee listings | `.org`, durable |
| Nashville nonprofit directories | Program listings | Citation plus link |
| Corporate sponsors of fight nights | Sponsor pages link back | Varied |

**[YOU] Quarterly:** pick two targets from the top three rows and pitch them.
Two good school or press links a quarter beats fifty directory submissions.

**[CLAUDE] Monthly:**

**Unlinked mentions are the cheapest links you will ever get.** Someone already
wrote about you. Asking them to hyperlink the name they already typed converts
at a rate no cold outreach touches.

### The one rule on the FBG and BRC cross-link

Both sites must describe the relationship the same way, using the same entity
names, and link to each other from a real page (About, Community, or Our Gyms),
not from a footer badge. Two organisations that genuinely partner should look
that way to a crawler. Two organisations telling different stories look like an
entity mess, which is what the audit originally flagged.

## 2. The content engine

The blog is **retained** and lives in the footer, not the top nav. It exists for
search engines and answer engines, not for a visitor who came to book a trial.

### Publish against proven shapes, not topics

Both gyms' existing content shows which formats actually work. Write to these
deliberately instead of publishing general fitness filler.

| Shape | What it earns | FBG examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Profile and lineage** | Editorial links | Halbert's Olympic and federation work; Evan Carr's coaching background; Sena Agbeko and the pro roster; the history of women's boxing |
| **Beginner on-ramp** | Local search | What to expect at your first class; what gear a beginner actually needs; how to wrap your hands |
| **Local reference** | Citations and AI answers | Expand `/amateur-boxing-in-tennessee/` into the definitive state guide: sanctioning, passbooks, where to compete, what a first bout costs |
| **Community and nonprofit** | Press links and shares | Hit the Books league results; Let Them Box; where membership money goes |

### Cadence

**One new post a month, and one existing post deepened.** Not four thin posts.
The single highest-value piece of content work available right now is turning
`/amateur-boxing-in-tennessee/` into the best resource of its kind in the state,
because it is simultaneously a local asset, a link magnet and an AI citation
source.

### Rules

- **Never invent a fact.** Names, records, dates, belt ranks, certifications and
  results come from the client. A wrong credential on a coach page destroys the
  exact authority the page exists to build, and the boxing community catches it.
- **Copy stays frozen at 99%** on anything client-supplied. Flag rewrites at
  handover.
- **Answer in the first 40 words.** That opening is what gets quoted in an AI
  answer. Question as the heading, answer immediately, expand after.
- **Cross-link posts from the program pages they relate to.** With the blog in
  the footer, contextual internal links are what keep posts from being orphaned.
  Keep every post in `sitemap.xml`.
- **Never repurpose a URL that holds a backlink** for a different subject.
  Expand it on the same subject instead.
- **No em dashes. Ever.** House rule.

## 4. AEO and GEO

Answer engines currently assemble Nashville boxing recommendations out of Yelp
lists, ClassPass roundups and city blogs. Fighters appears as a secondary
mention derived from those sources. The gym's own pages are not being quoted.

Two things change that:

1. **Be quotable.** Short, self-contained answers to real questions, question as
   the heading, answer in the first 40 words, `FAQPage` schema.
2. **Be the source those third parties cite.** The Tennessee amateur boxing
   guide and the nonprofit programs are the two assets other sites have a reason
   to link to and models have a reason to lean on.

Keep the entity graph clean and machine-readable: `Person` schema for every
coach with real `sameAs` links, `Organization` for the gym, an explicit
relationship to BRC, and consistent naming everywhere. Generative systems weight
named, resolvable entities heavily, and Halbert plus a registered nonprofit plus
a certified head coach is a graph almost no competing gym can produce.

**[CLAUDE] Quarterly AEO check:**

> Run these queries and report whether Fighters is cited, mentioned
> second-hand, or absent, and which sources the answer is built from: best
> boxing gym in Nashville; where to learn boxing in Nashville; boxing classes
> for beginners Nashville; women's boxing Nashville; youth boxing Nashville;
> amateur boxing Tennessee; boxing gym near The Nations. Compare against last
> quarter and tell me what moved.
