# SEO, the short version

## First: Rank Math will not work on this site

Rank Math is a **WordPress plugin**. This new site is not WordPress, so
Rank Math cannot be installed on it.

**Nothing is lost.** Everything Rank Math would have done, I already built
into the site directly. Rank Math's whole job is to make you fill in boxes
for every page. I filled those in, in the code, for all 25 pages. It now
happens by itself every time the site is rebuilt.

Keep Rank Math in mind for any site that stays on WordPress. For this one,
you do not need it.

## Was SEO "automated"? Sort of

Not magic. The honest version:

**Astro (the tool this site is built with) gives you for free:**
- A very fast site. Speed is a real ranking factor.
- Clean, simple code that search engines read easily.

**I had to write these, but now they happen automatically forever:**
- A title and description for every page (all 25)
- A sitemap that updates itself when pages are added
- "Schema", which is a hidden summary that tells Google and AI exactly
  what the business is: address, phone, classes, FAQs
- Correct links so nothing 404s

So: the plumbing is done and it maintains itself. That is the part software
can do.

## Your old site, for comparison

Your current site has an SEO plugin installed with **every single field
left blank**. All 34 pages. Zero titles, zero descriptions written.

Going from that to this is the single biggest and cheapest SEO win you
have. It is already done.

## What software cannot do

This is the part that actually brings in leads, and none of it is on the
website:

**1. Google Business Profile.** For a gym, this matters more than the
website. Claim it, fill in every field, primary category "Boxing gym",
post something weekly, add photos constantly.

**2. Reviews.** Review count is one of the strongest local ranking signals
and the biggest thing that makes someone choose you. Ask every member
after their first class. Reply to all of them. Never buy them.

**3. Your own story.** You have an **Olympic coach** who is a **hall of
fame inductee**, running a **nonprofit since 2001**, with a **pro boxer**
on staff. Almost no gym in Nashville can say that. Your current site
barely mentions it. Both Google and AI reward exactly this kind of real,
checkable credential. Putting it front and center is the biggest content
win available.

## Three things to do the week you launch

1. **Google Search Console.** Free. Add the site, submit the sitemap
   (`yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml`). This is how you see what people search
   to find you.
2. **Write down today's numbers.** Current leads per month, current
   traffic. Without a "before", you can never prove the "after".
3. **Start asking for reviews.** Today. It compounds.

## About doubling leads

Honest answer: leads will probably double from **conversion**, not
traffic.

If 100 people visit and 1 calls, that is 1 lead. If the site is clearer
and 2 call, you doubled your leads with the exact same traffic. The new
site has a popup form on every button, a real schedule, real coaches, and
a clear "what happens on your first day" page. That work is already done.

Doubling traffic takes months. Doubling conversion can happen the day you
launch.
