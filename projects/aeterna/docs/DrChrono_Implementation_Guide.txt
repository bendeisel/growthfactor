Aeterna Club & Compass Human Performance
Step-by-Step Build Guide for Staff
Version 2.0  •  2026-06-10  •  Aligned with CA v2026.6
How to Use This Guide
This document is a click-by-click implementation guide. Do not skip steps and do not invent your own sequence. Each section is numbered in the order it must be built. The entitlement tracker, smart phrases, and note templates depend on the appointment types, custom fields, and user roles being created first.
Intended users: Office Manager, Longevity Coordinators, RN, Front Desk. Each step flags which role owns it. The Medical Director reviews clinical templates once they are loaded.
Estimated time to execute: 32–40 staff-hours over 4 weeks. Do not attempt to complete in under a week. Build sequence is deliberate.
Build Sequence (Follow In Order)
Week 1 — Practice configuration, provider and user setup, appointment types, custom fields
Week 2 — Smart phrase library, note templates, entitlement tracker per tier
Week 3 — Reports, lab integration, pharmacy, PEMF rule enforcement, metric capture points
Week 4 — Clinical workflows rehearsal with test patient, GoHighLevel integration, staff training dry run
Success Criteria
A test patient can be onboarded end-to-end in under 90 minutes with no missing fields.
Every smart phrase fires correctly; every note template loads with prefilled fields.
The entitlement tracker reflects tier automatically upon patient creation.
Monthly coordinator, quarterly MD, and IV procedure notes complete cleanly with all required captures.
A member crossing any cap triggers an alert visible to Coordinator + Office Manager within 15 minutes.
All 14 cockpit metrics can be pulled from DrChrono reports without manual workarounds.
Section 1 — Practice Configuration
Role: Office Manager. Time: 90 minutes.
1.1 — Initial Practice Setup
Navigate to Account Settings → Practice Information. Complete each field:
Practice Name: enter the full legal name of the entity (Aeterna Club, LLC for AC; Compass Human Performance, LLC for CHP). Build these as two separate DrChrono practices — do not combine them in one account.
Practice Address, Phone, Fax, NPI (Type II): enter as registered with CMS.
Operational Hours: Monday–Friday 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM; Saturday 8:00 AM – 1:00 PM [CONFIRM with Lee].
Upload practice logo: high-resolution PNG, minimum 300 x 300 pixels, on letterhead and documents.
Enable HIPAA-compliant messaging via Patient Portal (Settings → Communication → Secure Messaging).
1.2 — Appointment Default Settings
Navigate to Settings → Scheduling.
Default appointment duration: 60 minutes.
Appointment buffer: 10 minutes between all appointments (critical for luxury pacing and modality reset).
Enable online patient self-scheduling: OFF for Aeterna Club (Coordinator-gated). ON for CHP with approval filter.
1.3 — Reminders Configuration
Navigate to Settings → Reminders. Configure two automated reminder rules:
SMS: 48 hours prior to appointment.
Email: 72 hours prior to appointment.
Reminder content (paste verbatim):
"Your Aeterna Club appointment is [Date] at [Time]. Please reply CONFIRM or call [number] to reschedule. We look forward to welcoming you."
1.4 — Lab Integration
Activate Quest Diagnostics eLab orders (Settings → Labs → Quest → Authenticate).
Activate LabCorp eLab orders (same path → LabCorp).
Configure in-house lab template for manual entry of BIA, VIBE, GSH, and Biocharger session data.
1.5 — Billing and E-Prescribing
Billing: Configure internal CPT tracking even though membership-based. This is critical for cost analysis and utilization reporting — without it, you cannot measure service margin per member.
E-Prescribing: Enable via DrFirst integration. Follow DrChrono's DrFirst setup wizard. Complete EPCS enrollment for controlled substances (required for peptides and HGH).
Enable Patient Portal for member secure messaging and document delivery.

3.7 — The PEMF Technology Rule
Critical scheduling rule: VEMI, Ammortal, and PEMF all use pulsed electromagnetic field technology. Only one of these three modalities may be used per day per member. If any one of the three is booked for a member on a given day, the other two MUST be blocked for that date. Enforce this at the scheduling layer via a custom rule or SOP discipline — DrChrono will not enforce it natively.

Section 5 — Entitlement Tracker (Core of Utilization Management)
Role: Office Manager. Time: 2–3 hours. This is the most critical section of the build.
The entitlement tracker is the single source of truth for what each member has used vs. what they are entitled to. Without it, you cannot defend pricing at renewal, you cannot identify upsell candidates, and you cannot prevent over-delivery (revenue leak).
5.1 — Build Approach in DrChrono
In Custom Fields, create a Patient Form called "Entitlement Tracker". Add it as a required form for every member.
For each row in the tier-specific tables below, create a row in the form with columns: Service | Annual Entitlement | Used YTD | Remaining | Module Allocation | Status.
Pre-populate Annual Entitlement and Module Allocation at the moment Tier is set in the Demographic fields — use DrChrono's Form Logic or, if unavailable, a triggered macro launched by the Coordinator at intake.
"Used YTD" must be incremented every time an IV/Procedure Note is saved for a capped service. See Template 4 (IV Infusion/Procedure Note) — the Entitlement Update section at the bottom feeds this field.
"Remaining" auto-calculates: Annual Entitlement − Used YTD.
"Status" is computed: Available (green) / Within 1 of cap (yellow) / Cap reached (red) / Add-on available (plus icon).
When Remaining = 1 for any capped service, fire an alert to Coordinator + Office Manager with template: "[Member Name] has 1 remaining [Service]. Discuss add-on or schedule final session." This is the single highest-leverage upsell mechanism in the entire system.

5.5 — Status Key and Cap Alert Logic
Green icon (checkmark): Available.
Yellow triangle icon: Within 1 of cap (alert triggered).
Red dot icon: Cap reached.
Plus icon: Add-on available (for services not included in tier).
Alert automation: when any capped service Remaining = 1, trigger a DrChrono internal message to Coordinator + Office Manager with this exact template: "[Member Name] has 1 remaining [Service]. Discuss add-on or schedule final session."

Section 11 — DrChrono to GoHighLevel Integration (via n8n)
Role: Dev team / Office Manager oversight. Time: 1–2 weeks of setup.
Five critical workflows to build in n8n. These keep DrChrono (clinical truth) and GoHighLevel (member relationship + sales pipeline) in sync bi-directionally.
11.1 — Workflow 1: New Member Sync
Trigger: Member closed in GHL.
Action: Create patient in DrChrono. Auto-populate entitlement tracker based on tier. Add to initial GHL pipeline stage: "Onboarding."
Validation: GHL Contact ID written back to DrChrono custom field.
11.2 — Workflow 2: Visit Completion Sync
Trigger: Appointment marked complete in DrChrono.
Action: Update GHL custom field "Last Visit Date" + append to "Visit Complete Log".
Purpose: Keep GHL aware of engagement without manual sync.
11.3 — Workflow 3: Weekly Health Score Calculation
Trigger: Every Monday 7:00 AM.
Action: Pull latest monthly check-in data from DrChrono. Recalculate health score per formula in Section 7. Update GHL custom field "Member Health Score". Also write to the Cockpit Google Sheet.
Critical: this is the main feed for the Cockpit. If this workflow fails, the Monday briefing is stale.
11.4 — Workflow 4: At-Risk Trigger Automation
Trigger (any of): missed 2+ visits | overdue labs | negative health score | score <60.
Action: Tag GHL contact with "At Risk". Move into Pipeline 4 (Retention). Notify Coordinator + Office Manager via email + Slack/Teams channel.
Follow-up: Coordinator has 24 hours to document At-Risk Action Note in DrChrono.
11.5 — Workflow 5: Renewal 90-Day Trigger
Trigger: 90 days before Membership End Date.
Action: Auto-mark renewal pipeline as "Due". Fire first renewal reminder (email + SMS). Generate renewal value report template pre-filled with member's YTD utilization, outcomes, add-on spend.
Follow-up cadence: 90 / 60 / 45 / 30 / 15 / 7 days, each with escalating personalization.
11.6 — Error Monitoring
Email alert to IT/Admin for any failed n8n workflow run.
Weekly dry run: Office Manager confirms all five workflows executed without error.
