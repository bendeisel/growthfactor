The number

Forty one enquiries. Nine replies.

I pulled a year of form submissions out of my old website because an agency wanted to sell me more traffic. Forty one people had asked us for help that year. We had replied to nine of them.

Not because we were lazy. Because the form went to an inbox on a laptop in the office, and I was on the floor from five to nine every night like every other owner in this industry. The leads were already there. We were the bottleneck, and the fix being sold to me was more of the thing I was already wasting.

I spent the next two years building the thing I actually needed: something that picked up when I could not, texted back when I missed, and kept going until somebody was booked in. Then other owners asked for it. That is the whole origin story, and there is no venture round or garage in it.

Why gyms only

Because a gym lead behaves nothing like a plumbing lead. They are nervous, they are comparing three places, they buy on Tuesday and go quiet until January. An agency serving nine industries writes one sequence for all of them. Ours is written for the person standing outside your door working up the nerve.

How we operate

Three rules we do not break.

These cost us money regularly. They are still the rules.

01

Under promise, over deliver

We quote fourteen days and aim for eleven. We never quote a number of leads, because anyone quoting you a number of leads is guessing and charging you for the guess.

02

Month to month, always

A twelve month contract is a company protecting itself from its own product. If we have to trap you to keep you, we have already failed and you should leave.

03

You own everything

Domain, site files, phone number, member list, ad accounts. All in your name. If you go, it goes with you and we will help you move it.
