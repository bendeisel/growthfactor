The rule

How it runs

Four conversations, one of them optional.

You’ll know the price before we touch anything.

You tell us what you want

A form or a twenty-minute call. What it should do, who it’s for, what already exists, and when you need it working.

We come back with a number and a scope

In writing: exactly what gets built, what it costs, how long it takes, and what we’d need from you. If it’s something you could genuinely build yourself in an hour, we’ll tell you that instead of taking the money.

We build it in your account

Not a copy, not a sandbox — your live account, so nothing has to be migrated at the end and nothing is hostage at any point.

We hand it over and show you the wiring

A recorded walkthrough of what we built and why, so you can change it yourself later. That handover is the point — we’d rather you never needed to hire us again.
