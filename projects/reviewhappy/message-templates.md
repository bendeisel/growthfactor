# Review Happy — Ask Timing & Message Copy

One ask per cycle. The bot handles everything after the customer replies.

## Timing by vertical

The trigger is the CRM event meaning "service happened". Delay is measured from it.

| Vertical | Trigger event | First ask | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurant / cafe / QSR | POS ticket closed or reservation completed | Next day, ~11am local | Same-day feels like being handed a comment card mid-meal. Next-day late morning catches them remembering it fondly. |
| Gym / studio / membership | Membership start date | Day 3, then day 21 | Day 3 captures onboarding enthusiasm; day 21 is after they've actually used it. |
| Home services (HVAC, plumbing, roof) | Job marked complete | Same day, 2–3 hours after | The technician is still fresh in mind and the problem is visibly fixed. |
| Med spa / dental / clinic | Appointment completed | Next day | Never in-office. Gives results a beat to settle. |
| Auto repair | Vehicle picked up | Same day, early evening | They've driven it and it works. |
| Real estate | Closing date | Day 2 | Day-of is chaos. |

## First-ask copy

Keep it one message. Business name up front so it isn't read as spam. Opt-out language is
not optional — it's what keeps the 10DLC campaign healthy.

**Restaurant**
> Hi {{first_name}} — {{business_name}} here. Thanks for coming in yesterday. If you've got
> 30 seconds, an honest Google review makes a real difference for a small place like ours:
> {{link}} — Reply STOP to opt out.

**Home services**
> Hi {{first_name}}, {{business_name}}. Glad we got that sorted today. If you'd share how it
> went, an honest Google review helps other neighbors find us: {{link}} — Reply STOP to opt out.

**Gym / membership**
> Hi {{first_name}} — {{business_name}}. You're three days in. If you'd tell people honestly
> how it's going so far, it helps a lot: {{link}} — Reply STOP to opt out.

**Clinic / med spa**
> Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{business_name}}. Thanks for coming in yesterday. If you're
> willing to share your honest experience on Google, we'd appreciate it: {{link}} — Reply
> STOP to opt out.

**Auto repair**
> Hi {{first_name}} — {{business_name}}. Hope it's driving right. An honest Google review
> helps other folks find a shop they can trust: {{link}} — Reply STOP to opt out.

## Re-ask copy (ask 2 and 3 only)

Minimum 30 days since the last ask, and only after a *new* service event. Never a standalone
nag with no new visit behind it.

> Hi {{first_name}} — {{business_name}} again. Thanks for coming back in. If you didn't get
> around to it last time, an honest Google review still helps: {{link}} — Reply STOP to opt out.

## The three-ask cap

Three lifetime asks, minimum 30 days apart, each tied to a real service event. Then the
contact goes dormant permanently.

This is not politeness. Repeated unsolicited review texts generate spam complaints, carrier
complaint rates trigger filtering, and sustained filtering gets a 10DLC campaign suspended.
If your clients share a campaign, one over-aggressive account takes SMS down for all of them.
The cap protects the book of business.
