01  /  Diagnosis

Three reasons the name-match gyms are winning

You are right that exact-name gyms rank ahead of you, and you are right that it is unfair. But the name is only one of three things beating you, and it is the only one you cannot change cheaply. Fix the other two and the name stops being decisive.

Reason 1Biggest, and fully yours to fix

KEYWORD CANNIBALISATION ACROSS FOUR PAGES

Covered above. /classes/Jiu-Jitsu and /classes/Brazilian-Jiu-Jitsu are two different pages competing for the same searcher, and /classes/New-Brazilian-Jiu-Jitsu makes three. This is the single largest thing holding the program back and it costs nothing but a decision to fix.

Reason 2Free, one afternoon

YOUR CATEGORY SAYS MUAY THAI, NOT JIU JITSU

Evidence

Yelp files you under Muay Thai as the lead category. If the Google Business Profile primary category follows the same logic (Mixed martial arts gym, or Martial arts school), you are telling Google you are not primarily a jiu jitsu school. Primary category is the strongest relevance lever in the local pack and you only get one.

The trade, stated plainly

Switching primary to a jiu jitsu category will help jiu jitsu queries and may soften MMA and Muay Thai queries. That is a real business decision, not a free win. You told me jiu jitsu is the target, so my recommendation is to make it primary and carry the rest as secondary categories. But make that call knowingly.

Reason 3Partly fixable

THE BUSINESS NAME HAS NO KEYWORD IN IT, AND THEIRS DO

Who you are up against

Artista Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Liberdade Jiu Jitsu, Legion Jiu Jitsu, Shawn Hammonds Jiu Jitsu Academy, Triangle Academy of Jiu-Jitsu, Renzo Gracie Nashville, Profectus Jiu-Jitsu, Gracie Barra Franklin. Most of those are genuine legal names, not spam, so there is nothing to report.

What I am not going to tell you to do

Do not stuff "Jiu Jitsu" into the Business Profile name. It violates the naming guideline, it is the most commonly reported local spam there is, and a suspension puts 485 reviews at risk. That review profile is the most valuable asset the gym owns. We are not gambling it for a keyword.

The legitimate version

You already own the answer and you are not using it. Bryan Tidwell is the founder and head black belt of the Matrix Jiu-Jitsu Association. That is a real brand with a real person behind it. If the BJJ program operates as "Matrix Jiu-Jitsu at Nashville MMA" with actual signage on the mat room, actual use in enrollment paperwork and actual usage in the wild, the name becomes defensible rather than invented. That is a months-long branding move, not a text edit, and it is the only compliant route to a keyword in the name. Worth discussing with the owners. Everything else in this document works without it.

THE LINEAGE PAGE, THE ONE THING A THREE-YEAR-OLD GYM CANNOT FAKE

BJJ has a formal, traceable lineage, and practitioners take it seriously enough to search for it. A page that traces Tidwell's promotion back through his professors to the Gracie line is content no new gym can produce, it is exactly what "true authority in Nashville" looks like written down, and it is the sort of page that gets cited and linked by the BJJ community rather than ignored.

What goes on it
  • The lineage chain itself, named professor by professor.
  • Every black belt the academy has promoted since 2002, with the year. This is the strongest authority claim a BJJ school can make and almost nobody publishes it.
  • Notable competition results by year.
  • The 2002 founding, with what the gym looked like then.
Three numbers to settle before any of this gets published

You said 25 years. The site says established 2002, which is 24. On a page whose entire job is being the verifiable authority, a number that does not add up undermines the thing you are claiming. My recommendation is to drop the round number and say "since 2002" everywhere, because it is checkable, it never goes stale, and it beats a competitor's vague "years of experience" outright.

Two more conflicts in the same family: the facility is described as both 30,000 and 40,000 square feet depending on the page, and class volume is quoted as both 80+ and 90+. Pick one of each and push it everywhere. Also worth fixing: Tapology spells him Brian Tidwell while your site says Bryan. Two spellings means two entities to a machine, which is precisely the connection we are trying to make.
