What martial arts disciplines do you offer?

We currently have Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (Gi and No Gi), Muay Thai, Boxing, Wrestling, and MMA classes! (As well as adult fitness classes!)

For kids, we have Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (Ages 6-14) and Kids Fitness (Ages 9-14).

What should you expect on your first day?

Come 15 minutes before your scheduled class time so we can give you a tour of the gym and introduce you to the coach leading your class! You also must sign the liability waiver before your class (must have a legal guardian sign if you are under 18 years old). You will enjoy a 60 min workout/skill-building experience with a community of our members sweating it out with you. Come hydrated and prepared to workout

What should I wear?

  • For all classes clean gear/clothes are required.
  • For our martial art classes, we do not wear shoes on the mat so slip-ons are recommended. We ask you to wear normal athletic clothes that do not have buttons, zippers, or pockets for all martial art classes.
  • Mouth Guards are recommended for anyone who wants to spar.
  • For our Jiu Jitsu Gi classes, a Gi and belt are required. The color of the gi is up to the student.
  • For Muay Thai, gloves and shin guards are required (Rental Gear Available)
  • Muay Thai and boxing both require boxing gloves. (16oz gloves are required if you are sparring)
  • Muay Thai and MMA both require shin guards.
  • MMA requires MMA gloves (hybrid/sparring MMA gloves with cushion on top of the knuckles)

Do I need to bring my own gear?

All students are required to have their own gear.

However, we provide rental equipment for free to anyone taking a trial class.

New members can also use our gear for free during the first two weeks of their membership. This gives students time to identify their preferred gear before purchasing their own. If you forget something, we have rentals available for $5 per class.

Do you have classes for all skill levels?

Yes! We have something for everybody!

Fitness: All levels and experiences are welcome in every one of our classes!

Martial Arts: Our martial art classes are split into different skills levels.

  • Advanced (Recommended 1+ years experience, more advanced drilling and skills)
  • Intermediate (3-6 months of experience, depending on training frequency. Consult a coach if you want to move up to the next level in your training. *Sparring starts at the intermediate level for our striking programs.)
  • All Levels (Everyone welcome, but more than just basics being taught, not recommended until student is familiar with basic terminology and can execute beginner skills)
  • Fundamentals (Everyone welcome, designed specifically for beginners, covering the basics of the martial art, and a great way to learn proper drilling techniques)

Is sparring or contact training involved in the martial arts classes?

In most of our martial art classes there is some sort of sparring or low-medium intensity drilling. We do not permit any sparring without a coach present. NMMATC has rules in regards to sparring and the coach is responsible to oversee and enforce the rules to help ensure everyone's safety. Safety gear is required based on the class. We encourage everyone to pay attention to their surroundings and to speak up if you are feeling uncomfortable. Communicate with your training partners and coaches if you feel someone is performing with too much power or they are acting careless on the mat. We want everyone in our gym to be challenged but safety comes first! Communicate your needs and take your training partners' needs into consideration.
