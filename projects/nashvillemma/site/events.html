SHOP LOCAL. SUPPORT A CAUSE. Community Vendor Event Benefiting Boxing Resource Center

  • Date: April 11th
  • Time: 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  • Location: 1504 Elm Hill Pike, Nashville, TN 37210
  • Hosted By: Nashville MMA Training Camp

What is this event? We are hosting a Shop Local event to support Boxing Resource Center. This event brings together small businesses, food vendors, music, and our local community while raising funds for an important cause.

Who are we supporting? Boxing Resource Center is a non-profit, tax-exempt 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to training youth and adults in Olympic-style boxing.

Their Mission: To empower youth and adults to enhance their lives, improve their physical and mental health, and engage with the community, one punch at a time.

What to Expect:

  • Local small business vendors
  • Food vendors on site
  • Music
  • Community engagement
  • Discounted donated gear available for purchase
  • Proceeds benefiting Boxing Resource Center

Who Can Attend? The event is open to the public. Families, community members, and supporters are all welcome.

How Can Businesses Participate? Small businesses are encouraged to purchase a vendor table and participate in the event.
