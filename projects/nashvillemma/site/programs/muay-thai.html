Muay Thai Classes In Nashville - 13 Every Week, Six Days A Week

Muay Thai is the national sport of Thailand and it is known as the art of eight limbs: fists, elbows, knees and shins. Most striking styles give you two weapons. Muay Thai gives you eight, and you will learn to use all of them!

We run 13 Muay Thai classes every week at Nashville MMA Training Camp, spread across six days. Morning Muay Thai classes start at 9:00 AM on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Evening classes run at 6:30 PM and 7:30 PM. Saturday has women's Muay Thai at 10:30 AM and an all-levels class at 11:30 AM. Eight of those 13 classes are Fundamentals, so if you are brand new there is almost always a beginner Muay Thai class waiting for you.

Our Muay Thai gym is at 1504 Elm Hill Pike, about ten minutes from downtown Nashville and right off Briley Parkway.

What Is Muay Thai, And How Is It Different From Kickboxing?

People ask us this every week! Kickboxing gives you punches and kicks. Muay Thai adds elbows, knees and the clinch, which is the standing grappling position where you control your partner's head and posture. That clinch is what makes Thai boxing different from every other striking art, and it is one of the most useful things you can learn for self-defense.

It is also the striking base for modern MMA. If you have watched a UFC card, you have watched Muay Thai. That is why our Muay Thai classes and our MMA program share so many students.

Your First Muay Thai Class

Come 15 minutes early so we can give you a tour and introduce you to the coach teaching that class. Here is what to bring:

Leave your shoes at the edge of the mat. Tell the coach it is your first day and they will pair you with somebody who will look after you. Nobody is going to throw you in with a fighter on day one.

Athletic clothes with no zippers and no pockets

A water bottle

Nothing else! Gear rental is free for your trial class, so you do not need to buy gloves or shin guards before you know you like it

Do I Have To Spar?

No. Sparring is always supervised and it is always your choice. Plenty of our Muay Thai students train for years and never spar, and plenty of others are on the fight team. Both are fine here. When you do want to start, a coach will bring you in slowly and with the right partners.

Who Trains Muay Thai Here

Our Muay Thai classes in Nashville run from complete beginners in their first week to active competitors. We have members in their twenties and members in their fifties, people training for fitness, people training for self-defense, and people training to fight. Class is structured so all of them get something out of the same hour.

You do not need to be in shape first. Come as you are and the conditioning arrives on its own.
