What Can You Expect From Our Muay Thai Classes At Nashville MMA Training Camp?

Muay Thai is a combat sport from Thailand that combines striking techniques using fists, elbows, knees, and shins. While Muay Thai is known for its dynamic and powerful strikes, intricate combinations, and emphasis on clinch work, our program also incorporates conditioning exercises to improve strength, endurance, and flexibility.

Participants will have opportunities to work with coaches and training partners, honing their strikes through pad drills and heavy bag sessions. This includes practicing combinations, defensive maneuvers, counters, and movement patterns. As students progress in their training, they will have the opportunity to engage in controlled sparring sessions and partner drills under the supervision of experienced coaches.

Nashville MMA Training Camp offers several different Muay Thai classes:

01

Muay Thai Fundamentals

In this class, you'll learn the fundamental techniques, principles, and movements of Muay Thai. We focus on proper stance, basic strikes, footwork, and defensive techniques. You'll develop a solid foundation in Muay Thai while building strength, agility, and coordination.

02

Muay Thai Intermediate

This class provides a well-rounded training experience, catering to the needs and goals of intermediate student. We cover a wide range of techniques, including punches, kicks, knees, elbows, clinch work, and combinations. Our experienced instructors provide modifications and progressions, ensuring that everyone can participate and continue to improve their skills.

03

Muay Thai Advanced

In this class, we focus on refining technique, advanced combinations, defensive strategies, and fight-specific conditioning. Our instructors, who have extensive experience in the ring, provide in-depth coaching and insights into high-level Muay Thai techniques and tactics. This class is perfect for fighters preparing for competitions or those who want to delve deeper into the intricacies of the martial art.
