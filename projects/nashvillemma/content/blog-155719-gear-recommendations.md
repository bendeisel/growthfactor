## Gear Recommendations

At Nashville MMA Training Camp , we want you training safely and confidently from day one. Below you’ll find the required gear for each program so you’re fully prepared for class.

### Boxing Classes

Required Gear:

• 16 oz Boxing Gloves

16 oz gloves provide the proper protection for bag work, partner drills, and controlled sparring.

### Muay Thai Classes

Required Gear:

• 16 oz Boxing Gloves • Shin Pads

Muay Thai includes punches and kicks, making shin protection essential for safe partner training.

### Jiu Jitsu Classes

Required Gear:

• Gi (Competition Gi must be black, blue, or white) • Rashguards • Shorts with no pockets or zippers

Proper attire ensures safety, mobility, and compliance with competition standards.

### MMA Classes

Required Gear:

• Sparring Gloves (Muffin Top Gloves) • Shin Pads

MMA blends striking and grappling, requiring hybrid sparring gloves and shin protection.
