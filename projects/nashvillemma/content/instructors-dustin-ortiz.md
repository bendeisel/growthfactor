## Dustin Ortiz

Dustin Ortiz is an accomplished MMA coach with 18 years of martial arts experience. With roots in Goshen, Indiana, and Franklin, Tennessee, Dustin holds a professional MMA record of 20-9 and competed in major promotions including UFC, Strikeforce, and King of the Cage. Dustin is a black belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, notably placing third in the No-Gi World Championships.

Dustin emphasizes an "all or nothing" approach, aiming to instill determination and resilience in his students. He notably holds the record for the fastest knockout in UFC Flyweight history.

Off the mats, Dustin enjoys bull riding and other adrenaline-filled activities.

Join Coach Dustin Ortiz for world-class MMA training and unmatched dedication.
