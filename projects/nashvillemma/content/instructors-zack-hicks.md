## Zack Hicks

Zack Hicks, a dedicated MMA coach from Clarksville, Tennessee, brings 16 years of martial arts experience. A seasoned competitor with a professional record of 9–5 and an amateur record of 15–4, he has twice competed in the PFL and captured titles with Valor, SSF, ICF, and Hardrock MMA.

Zack is currently a high school teacher and a former high school wrestling coach with 8–10 years of coaching experience, infusing his sessions with classroom clarity and mat-tested fundamentals. His coaching philosophy emphasizes consistency, compassion, and technical precision.

Train with Coach Zack Hicks to enhance your technical skills and elevate your fighting potential.
