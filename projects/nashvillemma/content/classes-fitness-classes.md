#### What Types Of Fitness Training & Exercise Classes Do We Offer?

Whether you're here in Nashville or nearby, we're proud to be your source for high-quality fitness training. Our diverse range of fitness classes will help you choose what's right for your body, your goals, and your lifestyle, all while enjoying a fun and high-energy setting!

Just check out the variety of exercise classes and fitness training options we have to offer:

Sports Performance Class - Train just like the PROS!

Elevate your performance with Nashville MMA’s Sports Performance class, engineered for athletes and fitness enthusiasts who want to train with purpose and precision. Inspired by Co-Owner UFC Lightweight Contender Michael Chandler, who is renowned for his explosive athleticism and training protocols, our program will help build you into a high-level athlete! Using plyometric, medicine ball power work, compound lifts, and bodyweight movements, you’ll feel more explosive and athletic in no time!

Benefits of professional-style training:

- Develop speed, power, and reactive strength

- Enhance coordination and conditioning

- Increase muscular endurance and functional strength

- Improve metabolic efficiency for superior conditioning

- Train using evidence-based methods used by elite MMA fighters and professional athletes

Whether you’re an aspiring competitor or a dedicated fitness enthusiast, our Sports Performance class delivers the tools you need to perform at the highest level. Check out our schedule to see class times.

Iron Pile Class - Strength Training for Peak Performance

Start your day with power and purpose in Iron Pile, Nashville MMA’s premier morning strength training class. This program is designed for individuals who want to build real strength, increase muscle mass, and optimize body composition. Through progressive weightlifting techniques, compound lifts, and strategic training cycles, Iron Pile helps you build real strength, boost your metabolism, to feel stronger and more confident—both in your workouts and everyday life.

Benefits of Iron Pile training:

- Increase lean muscle mass while accelerating fat loss

- Improve bone density, joint stability, and injury resilience

- Boost metabolic rate for sustained calorie burn

- Build maximal strength through compound and accessory movements

- Train using science-backed strength protocols for measurable results

If you’re serious about transforming your physique and performance, Iron Pile at Nashville MMA is where you belong.

Join us and lift your way to a stronger, leaner you. Check out our schedule to see class times.

Personal Training: Our certified personal trainers in Nashville are available to create customized fitness programs tailored to your specific goals, whether it's weight loss, muscle gain, improved endurance, or sports-specific training. With personalized attention and expert guidance, you'll benefit from focused workouts, proper form correction, and ongoing motivation to keep you on track. Some of our personal trainers offer nutritional guidance to complement your training efforts, helping you make informed choices about your diet and fueling your body for optimal performance.

Open Gym: For those who prefer self-directed workouts, our open gym provides you with access to our cutting-edge equipment and facilities. Work on your own routines, practice techniques, or engage in cardio sessions at your own pace. Our staff is on hand to offer advice and assistance if needed. Open gym is also included in all of our new martial arts memberships.
