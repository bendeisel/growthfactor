## Shawn Allen

Shawn Allen from Chattanooga, TN, brings two decades of MMA experience to Nashville MMA Training Camp, coaching for 18 years and competing extensively. With an amateur record of 16-3-1 and professional fights across prestigious promotions, Shawn continues to compete actively in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu tournaments.

A purple belt in BJJ, Shawn uses a constraints-led, ecological dynamics approach to coaching, emphasizing realistic, scenario-based training. His method develops adaptive, resilient athletes skilled in rapid decision-making.

Outside the gym, Shawn is known for his handyman skills and holds certifications in USA Weightlifting, NASM, and CrossFit.

Train with Coach Shawn Allen to develop real-world self-defense skills, athleticism, and mental agility.
