## Kevin Patterson

Kevin Patterson brings 18 years of extensive martial arts experience to Nashville MMA Training Camp. A native of Nashville, Kevin is a black belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and holds a BA in Psychology from UNC Chapel Hill. His athletic accolades include two-time Tennessee State Decathlon Champion and certifications in Functional Range Conditioning and Precision Nutrition.

Kevin’s approach to coaching is systems-based, detail-oriented, and contextual, encouraging students to understand techniques and concepts deeply. His challenging class environment helps students achieve their unique fitness and martial arts goals.

Outside of martial arts, Kevin enjoys DIY projects, real estate, and playing various sports.

Train with Coach Kevin Patterson and take ownership of your martial arts journey.
