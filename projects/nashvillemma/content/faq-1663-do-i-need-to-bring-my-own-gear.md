# Do I need to bring my own gear?

### Request More Information

[Button: Request More Information](#)

All students are required to have their own gear.

However, we provide rental equipment for free to anyone taking a trial class.

New members can also use our gear for free during the first two weeks of their membership. This gives students time to identify their preferred gear before purchasing their own. If you forget something, we have rentals available for $5 per class.
