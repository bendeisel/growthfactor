## Women’s Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu: Learn To Defend Yourself With Technique — Not Strength

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu teaches leverage, control, and submissions that allow you to neutralize a larger opponent. In our women-only BJJ classes, you’ll learn:

- Sweeps and submissions

- Takedown fundamentals

- Real-world ground defense

Our structured program ensures beginners feel comfortable while experienced students continue progressing.

## Women’s Muay Thai Classes: Build Power, Conditioning & Confidence

Muay Thai, known as the “Art of Eight Limbs,” combines punches, kicks, knees, and elbows into one of the most effective striking systems in the world. In our women’s Muay Thai classes in Nashville, you’ll train:

- Proper stance and footwork

- Pad work and heavy bag drills

- Combinations and defensive techniques

- Full-body conditioning

Every class challenges you physically and mentally while helping you develop real striking skills.
