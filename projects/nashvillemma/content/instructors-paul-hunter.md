## Paul Hunter

Paul Hunter is an experienced Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu coach with nearly 7 years of dedicated training and coaching experience at Nashville MMA Training Camp. Originally from Tennessee, Paul has built an impressive competition record, participating in 23 BJJ tournaments and securing 9 gold, 7 silver, and 3 bronze medals. A proud purple belt holder, Paul emphasizes that "advanced Jiu Jitsu is fundamental Jiu Jitsu done extremely well."

Paul's coaching philosophy is deeply rooted in fostering a passion for Jiu-Jitsu among his students, creating a supportive and motivating environment. His dedication to fitness and martial arts is evident, boasting peak fitness at the age of 56.

Join Coach Paul Hunter to discover the fundamentals of Jiu-Jitsu and ignite your passion for martial arts.
