# Strength Training and Sports Performance in Nashville

## Sports Performance - Our 6:00 AM Group Exercise Class

Sports Performance is our group exercise class at Nashville MMA Training Camp, and it runs at 6:00 AM Monday through Friday. Five mornings a week, one class, before work. That is the group exercise class on our schedule right now and we put everything into it.

This is strength training and conditioning built around real weightlifting, not a circuit of machines. You will squat, you will deadlift, you will press, you will pull a sled, and you will leave before 7:00 AM.

#### What Happens In The Class

Every Sports Performance class follows the same shape:

- A real warm up: mobility, activation and something to get your heart rate up
- The strength piece: a main weightlifting movement, worked in progressive cycles so the numbers actually go up
- Accessory work: the smaller lifts that keep your shoulders, hips and back healthy
- A conditioning finisher on the turf, the sled, the rower, the ski erg or the bike

Coaches run the whole hour. They will set your weights, fix your form and tell you when to add plates and when to back off. That is the difference between a group exercise class and a gym membership where you guess.

#### Why Weightlifting Is The Base

We build this class on weightlifting because nothing else does as much. Weightlifting is what adds lean muscle, protects your joints, keeps your bone density up as you get older and raises your metabolism for the rest of the day. Cardio is good for you. Weightlifting changes what your body is made of.

Our weightlifting programming moves in cycles rather than random workouts. You repeat the main lifts often enough to get better at them, with the weight climbing week to week. That is how strength training is supposed to work, and it is the part most group exercise classes skip.

#### Who This Group Exercise Class Is For

Everybody. You do not have to be a fighter and you do not have to train martial arts at all. We have members who only come to the 6:00 AM group exercise class and never step on the mats!

- People who want to get strong and are tired of guessing in a commercial gym
- Athletes in other sports who need real strength and conditioning work
- Martial arts members adding weightlifting to their jiu jitsu, boxing or Muay Thai
- Anybody who wants their workout done before the day starts

New to weightlifting? Say so on your first morning. The coach will scale every movement and start you where you actually are.

## The Gym Side: 40,000 Square Feet, And Two Turf Areas

Our facility is 40,000 square feet. The gym side of it is a full weight room that most martial arts gyms simply do not have, and it is bigger and better equipped than plenty of standalone gyms in Nashville.

Here is what is on the floor:

- Power racks, barbells, bumper plates and a full set of free weights
- Dumbbells all the way up to 100 lb
- Cable machines for upper and lower body
- **Two separate sections of turf**, so a class can run on one while you work on the other
- Sleds to push and pull
- Sandbags, sandballs and medicine balls
- Concept 2 rowers and ski ergs
- Rogue Echo bikes and spin bikes
- Stairmaster and treadmill

The sleds and the turf are the part you do not get at a normal gym. Most commercial gyms in Nashville have rows of machines and nowhere to actually move. We have two turf lanes, so you can sled push, carry, sprint and drag without waiting for anybody or apologising for taking up space. That is why our conditioning finishers look nothing like a regular gym's.

You also get the Recovery Room with an adult membership: dry heat sauna, cold tub, Normatec compression boots and a mobility area. Train hard in the morning, sit in the sauna, go to work.
