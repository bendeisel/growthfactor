# Muay Thai Classes in Nashville

## Thirteen Muay Thai Classes A Week

Muay Thai is the art of eight limbs: two fists, two elbows, two knees, two shins. Learning it means learning to throw with all of them, and to read what is coming back at you.

We run it thirteen times a week across six days, at three levels, plus a women's class on Saturday morning. Mornings go at 9:00. Everything else sits at 6:30 and 7:30 in the evening. You should not have to rearrange your life to train, so we built the week around that.

#### What A Round Actually Looks Like

Your first few weeks are stance, footwork, and how to throw a kick without losing your balance. Then pads. A coach holding Thai pads will tell you more about your technique in three minutes than an hour of shadowboxing ever will.

After that it stacks: combinations, defense, counters, clinch. Sparring is controlled, supervised, and entirely up to you. Nobody here gets thrown to the wolves in week two.

- Fundamentals: Stance, the basic strikes, footwork, defensive shapes. Eight of our thirteen weekly classes sit at this level, so a beginner is never waiting around for a slot to open.
- Intermediate: Friday evenings. Longer combinations, clinch work, and the counters that come after somebody blocks you.
- Advanced: Monday, Tuesday and Thursday at 6:30. Fight-specific conditioning and the tactical side, coached by people who have competed.
- Women's Muay Thai: Saturday at 10:30 AM, women only.

#### The Conditioning Comes Whether You Ask For It Or Not

Thai pads are cardio you cannot fake your way through. Six rounds in, your legs are gone and your hands are either still up or they are not. That gap is the thing the sport actually trains.

You will get fitter. You will also stop flinching, which is harder to put on a poster and worth more.

#### Come In And Hit Pads

One class will tell you more than this page can. Wear athletic clothes with no zippers or pockets, bring water, and leave the shoes at the edge of the mat. Gear rental is free for your trial class, so there is nothing to buy first.

Fill out the form and we will tell you which class to start in.
