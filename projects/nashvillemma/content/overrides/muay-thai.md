# Muay Thai Classes in Nashville

## Learn Muay Thai in Nashville - 13 Classes Every Week

Muay Thai is known as the art of eight limbs - fists, elbows, knees and shins. You'll learn to use all of them!

We run 13 Muay Thai classes a week across six days, so you can find a time that works. Morning classes start at 9:00 AM and evening classes run at 6:30 and 7:30 PM.

#### What To Expect In Your First Class

Come 15 minutes early so we can give you a tour and introduce you to the coach leading your class! Wear athletic clothes with no zippers or pockets, bring water, and leave your shoes at the edge of the mat. Gear rental is free for your trial class, so there is nothing you need to buy first.

Your first few weeks cover stance, footwork and the basic strikes. From there you'll work pads with a coach, then combinations, defense and clinch work. Sparring is always supervised and always your choice - you will never be pushed into it.

#### Our Muay Thai Classes

- Fundamentals: Stance, basic strikes, footwork and defense. Eight of our 13 weekly classes are at this level, so beginners always have a spot!
- Intermediate: Friday evenings. Longer combinations, clinch work and counters.
- Advanced: Monday, Tuesday and Thursday at 6:30 PM. Fight-specific conditioning and strategy with coaches who have competed.
- Women's Muay Thai: Saturday mornings at 10:30 AM, women only!
