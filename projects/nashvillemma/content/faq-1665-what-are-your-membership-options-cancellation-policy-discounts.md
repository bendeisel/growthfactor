# What are your membership options/cancellation policy/discounts?

### Request More Information

[Button: Request More Information](#)

Our memberships are based on how often you plan on training! Please contact our front desk team to find the best option for you!

Our recovery room is included in all adult memberships.

All of our memberships are month-to-month with a 28-day cancellation policy. For all membership adjustments, holds, cancellations, or upgrades/downgrades, please visit https://memberships.nashvillemma.com/changes .

We do offer military, first responder, family, and student discounts. (Sorry only one discount per account) Please bring identification to have your discount applied at the front desk.
