## Justin Weber

Justin Weber, originally from Chicago, brings over two decades of wrestling experience to Nashville MMA Training Camp. A former Big Ten wrestler at the University of Wisconsin, Justin was a 3rd-place state finisher in Illinois and named Illinois Scholastic Wrestler of the Year.

His coaching philosophy is built on intensity, adaptation, and hands-on learning. Justin believes that live practice and play-based exploration are essential to understanding movement and becoming a better fighter. His sessions reflect the mindset that "the guy in the best shape will eventually be the best."

When he’s not coaching, Justin channels his creativity into writing movie and TV screenplays with his brother.

Train with Coach Justin Weber to sharpen your wrestling game and experience a high-energy, results-driven training environment.
