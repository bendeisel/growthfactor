# What should I wear?

### Request More Information

[Button: Request More Information](#)

- For all classes clean gear/clothes are required.

- For our martial art classes, we do not wear shoes on the mat so slip-ons are recommended. We ask you to wear normal athletic clothes that do not have buttons, zippers, or pockets for all martial art classes.

- Mouth Guards are recommended for anyone who wants to spar.

- For our Jiu Jitsu Gi classes, a Gi and belt are required. The color of the gi is up to the student.

- For Muay Thai, gloves and shin guards are required (Rental Gear Available)

- Muay Thai and boxing both require boxing gloves. (16oz gloves are required if you are sparring)

- Muay Thai and MMA both require shin guards.

- MMA requires MMA gloves (hybrid/sparring MMA gloves with cushion on top of the knuckles)
