## Jim Casey

Jim Casey has dedicated over 31 years to coaching martial arts, including 20 years at Nashville MMA Training Camp. Originally from Geneseo, NY, Jim is a third-degree black belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, with a remarkable competitive record—holding over 75 tournament titles, including multiple Pan American and World IBJJF no-gi championships.

A licensed psychologist with a PsyD, Jim integrates mental discipline and strong character-building into his coaching. He emphasizes foundational skills, work ethic, and character development, believing champions are defined by their character both on and off the mats.

An Army veteran and collegiate wrestler, Jim values camaraderie, discipline, and lifelong martial arts training.

Join Coach Jim Casey for elite-level grappling instruction and character development that extends beyond martial arts.
