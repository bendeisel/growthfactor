#### Get In Fighting Shape With Our Wrestling Program

Wrestling requires significant physical strength, endurance, and agility. Our wrestling program in Nashville will incorporate specific conditioning exercises, strength training routines, and cardio workouts to improve participants' overall fitness levels and prepare them for the physical demands of wrestling.

The program will also include a variety of wrestling-specific drills to enhance students’:

- Technique, agility, and coordination

- Mental acuity and strategy

- Wrestling match tactics, positioning, setups, and counters

For participants interested in competing in wrestling events or transitioning to MMA, the program will focus on competition preparation. This may include simulated matches, specific training for wrestling tournaments, advice on weight management, and mental preparation techniques.
