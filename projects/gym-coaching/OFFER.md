# Gym coaching, $297/month

The switch from doing it for them to teaching them to do it. One price, one
cohort, four AI systems the gym owns at the end.

## The pitch in one line

You are already spending $3,101 a month on ads across three gyms and paying
between $6.42 and $16.19 for the same lead. The coaching costs 10% of that
spend and exists to close the gap.

## What $297 a month buys

| | What it is | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | 90 minutes, one to one. Connect the ad accounts, pull the baseline, build one ad together in AdKit while you watch. | Once, week 0 |
| **Weekly call** | 60 minutes, whole cohort, same time every week. Hot seats, account reviews, whatever is on fire. | Every week |
| **The modules** | Eight guided builds. You do the building, on your own account, at your own pace. | Async |
| **The four systems** | Lead follow-up bot, front desk agent, review engine, ad build. Yours, in your own GHL and your own ad account. | Built in modules 2, 6, 7, 5 |
| **Monthly numbers** | Your baseline re-pulled and compared. Spend, leads, CPL per campaign, what moved. | Monthly |
| **The channel** | Async questions between calls. | Ongoing |

## What it does not include

Stated up front, because the whole model dies if this bleeds.

- **We do not run your ads.** You run them. We build the first one with you and
  review the rest on the call.
- **We do not answer your leads.** Your bot does, and you own the bot.
- **Website is separate.** $100/month, up to 10 pages.
- **AEO, GEO and technical SEO are quoted separately**, $200 to $500 depending
  on scope. Local SEO and basic on-page SEO are already in the website plan.
- **Ad spend is paid by you, directly to the platform.** It never runs through
  us and it is not part of the $297.

## The four systems, in build order

1. **Lead follow-up bot** (module 2). GHL Conversation AI answers and books
   inbound leads day and night. 279 leads a month are already arriving across
   the cohort with no measured response time, so this is first.
2. **Ad build in AdKit** (module 5). Your own project, your own account, your
   campaigns built and reported in one place.
3. **Front desk agent** (module 6). Schedule, pricing and trial questions
   answered on the site and on social.
4. **Review engine** (module 7). The Review Happy build: seven custom fields, a
   three-ask lifetime cap 30 days apart, and a hard rule against gating.
   Compliance is not optional here, see `projects/reviewhappy/README.md`.

## Why the price works

At $297 the human time has to be bounded or the program becomes a job.

- Onboarding is 90 minutes, once, per member.
- The weekly call is 60 minutes and does not get longer with more members.
- Everything else is async and reusable.

That is **4 hours of live time a month regardless of headcount**, plus 90
minutes per new member. Twenty members is $5,940 a month against roughly 4 hours
of calls and 30 hours of one-time onboarding spread across the quarter.

The failure mode is the same one that kills $100 websites: support minutes.
If a member's question is answered in a module, the answer is a link, not a
call. If it is not in a module yet, answering it on the weekly call makes it a
module.

All measured, all in `baseline-2026-09.md`, all from the operator's own accounts:

- Furst Place buys a martial arts lead for **$6.42**.
- Nashville MMA's Kids campaign buys the same lead for **$16.19**, while having

Do not round these, do not annualize them into a bigger number, and do not
promise them to a new gym. They are what happened, not what will happen.
