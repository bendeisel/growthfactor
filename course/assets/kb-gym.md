# Knowledge Base Template: General Gym

Paste each section into HighLevel as its own Knowledge Base source, so a bad
answer can be traced to one section and fixed without touching the rest.

Recommended source types:

- **Rich Text** for sections 1 to 8. Written prose, easy to edit.
- **Tables** for the schedule and the pricing grid. Tables beat prose for
  anything an agent has to look up precisely.
- **FAQ** for section 9. One question, one answer.
- **Web Crawler** pointed at the gym's own site, as backup only.
- **File Upload** for the waiver, the member handbook and the price sheet.

Anything in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]` is for the owner to replace. An agent that
answers with a square bracket still in it is an agent that went live too early.

## 9. FAQ

Use the FAQ source type. Start with these, then add every real question a
member actually asks. Twenty real FAQs beat two hundred invented ones.

- What should I expect on my first day?
- What should I wear?
- Do I need to be fit before I start?
- Do you have classes for all skill levels?
- Is there parking?
- Are there showers?
- Is personal training available and what does it cost?
- What is the schedule?
- What equipment and facilities do you have?
- Can I freeze my membership?
- What is your cancellation policy?
- Do you do family or partner rates?

## 10. Rules for the agent

This section is not about the gym. It is the behaviour contract, and it is the
part most people skip and then complain the bot went off script.

**Always:**

- Answer from this knowledge base. If the answer is not here, say you will
  check with the team and hand off. Never guess.
- Keep replies under `[N]` sentences for SMS.
- Sign off as `[NAME OR THE GYM]`.

**Never:**

- Quote a price, discount, or contract term that is not written above.
- Give medical, injury, nutrition or weight loss advice.
- Promise a result, a timeline, or a body change.
- Argue with a complaint. Acknowledge it and hand to a human.
- Say you are a human if asked directly. `[DECIDE: how the gym wants the agent
  to describe itself, and write the exact sentence here]`

**Hand to a human immediately when:**

- They are upset, complaining, or mention a refund or a lawyer.
- They mention an injury, a medical condition, or a pregnancy.
- They ask for a discount or a custom deal.
- They ask something this base does not answer, twice.
- They ask to speak to a person.
