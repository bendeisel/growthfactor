# The 30 Questions

Send this to a gym before their agents go live. No agent gets published until
this comes back filled in. An agent with an empty knowledge base is worse than
no agent, because it answers confidently and wrongly, in public, to a lead.

Delivery: send it as a HighLevel form so answers land on the contact record,
or as a shared doc for gyms who would rather talk it through on a call.
The form takes most owners 25 minutes.

## The gym

1. Gym name, exactly as you want it written in messages.
2. Full address, plus the parking situation and any quirk about finding the door.
3. Staffed hours, and access hours if they differ.
4. In one sentence a member would actually say, what makes you different?
5. Who is your gym not for? Be blunt.

## What you sell

6. List every program you run, with the length of a session.
7. Which program should a total beginner start with?
8. What is your current trial or intro offer, in full?
9. Who qualifies for it, and who does not?
10. Walk me through a first visit, minute by minute.
11. Every membership plan: price, billing period, contract length, what is included.
12. Is the agent allowed to say prices? All of them, the starting price only, or none?
13. Joining fee, freeze policy, cancellation notice, refund policy.
14. What discounts exist that the agent may offer? If none, say none.

## Your people

15. Every coach: name, what they coach, credentials, one human detail about them.
16. Who does a lead get handed to when the agent escalates? Name, and how they want to be notified.
17. What hours is that person actually reachable?

## Kids, if you run kids programs

18. Age brackets and how classes split.
19. Do parents watch?
20. What does a child wear to a first class?
21. What is the kids trial offer?

## Martial arts, if that is you

22. Disciplines you teach.
23. Ranking system, the order of ranks, and roughly how long each takes.
24. Affiliation or lineage.
25. When does a new person first spar, and is it optional?
26. Gear they need to start, gear they need later, and rough cost.

## Rules

27. What must the agent never say? List everything, including topics you do not want touched.
28. How should the agent describe itself if someone asks whether it is a robot? Give the exact sentence.
29. What are your quiet hours? No agent messages before or after.
30. Give me the five questions you are sickest of answering. Those become the first five FAQs.

## After it comes back

- Fill the KB template, replace every square bracket, then search the document
  for `[` to confirm none survived.
- Anything they left blank becomes an escalation rule, not a guess.
- Question 30 is the most valuable answer on the form. Build the FAQ source
  from it first.
