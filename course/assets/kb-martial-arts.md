# Knowledge Base Template: Martial Arts Gym

Same structure as `kb-gym.md`. Sections 1, 2, 7, 8 and 10 carry over almost
unchanged, so start from that file and replace sections 3 through 6 and 9 with
what follows. The differences are not cosmetic: an MMA gym gets asked entirely
different questions from a strength gym, and an agent trained on the general
template will fumble every one of them.

## 9. FAQ, martial arts version

- Do I need to be fit before I start?
- Will I get hit on my first day?
- I have never done this before, will everyone else be better than me?
- Do I need a gi, and when?
- What do I wear to the first class?
- How long does it take to get a blue belt?
- Do I have to compete?
- Do I have to spar?
- Is it safe for my child?
- What ages do you take?
- Can I train if I am `[40 / 50 / overweight / recovering from something]`?
- Do you have a women's only class?
- Can I try one class before I commit?
- Do you accept my rank from my old school?
- What is the difference between gi and no-gi?
- Is there parking?

## 10 addendum. Extra rules for a martial arts agent

Add these to the behaviour contract in `kb-gym.md` section 10.

**Never:**

- Compare the gym to a named competitor school, ever. This community is small
  and it always gets screenshotted.
- Comment on another school's lineage, credentials, or quality.
- Predict how fast someone will rank up.
- Tell a parent their child will be able to defend themselves by a date.
- Give any advice about training around an injury.

**Always:**

- Treat "will I get hurt" and "is it safe for my kid" as the real question
  behind most enquiries, and answer it directly rather than pivoting to the
  offer.
- Ask whether the enquiry is for an adult or a child before anything else.
  Everything downstream changes on that answer.
