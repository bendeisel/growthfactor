# 10.1 Connecting Meta and Google

**Module:** 10 Ad Manager
**Video:** ~6 min
**Needs first:** modules 03, 08, 09
**You finish with:** ad accounts connected and leads flowing into the CRM

## Why this matters

Ad Manager runs Meta and Google campaigns from inside HighLevel, so you are
not toggling between Meta Business Suite, Google Ads and your CRM.

The bigger win is not the convenience. It is that a lead from an ad lands in
your CRM instantly and an agent answers it in under a minute. See 10.4.

## Before you connect anything

**Do not turn ads on until:**

- Your calendar has real availability. Module 03
- Your knowledge base is filled. Module 08
- Your appointment agent is published and tested. Module 09

Paying for leads that land in a system that cannot answer them is the most
expensive mistake available to you. Ads amplify what you have. If what you
have is unfinished, they amplify that.

## What you need

For Meta:

- A Facebook Page for your gym
- A Meta ad account, with billing set up
- Admin access to both
- Your Meta account not restricted

For Google:

- A Google Ads account with billing
- Ideally a verified Google Business Profile

## Steps

1. Open **Marketing > Ad Manager**, or the Integrations area.

   [SHOT 10.1-01]

2. Connect Meta. Log in with the account that has admin on the page and the
   ad account.

3. Select the ad account and the page.

   [SHOT 10.1-02]

4. Grant the permissions asked for. If you decline any, something will not
   work later and it will not be obvious which.

5. Connect Google Ads the same way.

6. Confirm both show as connected.

## The lead form connection

Separate from the ad account connection and the one people miss.

Meta lead ads need permission to send lead data through. Confirm that lead
form access is granted, then test it, per 10.4.

A campaign can run perfectly, spend money, generate leads, and none of them
reach your CRM because this one permission was skipped. You find out a week
later.

[SHOT 10.1-03]

## Which platform for a gym

If you can only do one, **Meta**.

| | Meta | Google |
|---|------|--------|
| How it works | Interrupts people who were not looking | Catches people already searching |
| Cost per lead | Usually lower | Usually higher |
| Lead quality | More variable | Usually higher intent |
| Best for | Offers, challenges, kids programs | "gym near me", "bjj classes [town]" |
| Creative needed | Yes, and it matters most | Text mainly |

Gyms are an impulse and emotion purchase as much as a search purchase. Meta
generally wins on volume and cost. Google wins on intent.

Start with Meta. Add Google when Meta is working and you want more.

## Checklist

- [ ] Calendar, knowledge base and agents are all done first
- [ ] Meta ad account and page connected
- [ ] Lead form permission granted
- [ ] Google Ads connected, if using it
- [ ] Billing set up on the ad platforms themselves

## When it goes wrong

**Connection fails.** Almost always permissions. You need admin on both the
page and the ad account, and they are separate things.

**Connected but no ad account showing.** The logged-in user does not have
access to that ad account.

**Leads not arriving.** The lead form permission. See 10.4.

**Meta account restricted.** Nothing to do with HighLevel. Sort it in Meta
Business Suite first.

## Video script

**Hook.** Before you connect anything: if your calendar is empty or your
knowledge base is half done, ads will just make your problems more expensive.

**Beats.**
1. On screen: talk to camera. The three prerequisites. Be firm about it.
2. On screen: connect Meta. Steady, show the page and ad account selection.
3. On screen: the lead form permission. Dwell here. Say plainly: skip this and
   you will spend a week's budget generating leads you never see.
4. On screen: the Meta versus Google table. Recommend Meta first.

**Go do.** Connect Meta, then confirm the lead form permission is granted.
Do not build a campaign yet.

## Verify on screen

- Exact nav path for Ad Manager.
- The permission list Meta asks for and which are required for lead delivery.
- Whether Google Ads campaign creation is supported or only reporting.
