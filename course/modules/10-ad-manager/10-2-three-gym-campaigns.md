# 10.2 The three gym campaigns worth running

**Module:** 10 Ad Manager
**Video:** ~8 min
**Needs first:** 10.1
**You finish with:** knowing what to run, in what order, and what to expect

## Why this matters

Gym owners waste money on ads by running the wrong thing: a generic brand
awareness campaign, a boosted post, an ad for "memberships" with no offer.

Three campaigns work for gyms. Run them in this order.

## Campaign 1: The offer lead form

**Run this first, and if you only run one, run this.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Objective | Leads |
| Format | Lead form on Meta, not a link to your site |
| Offer | Your trial, challenge, or intro offer |
| Audience | Local, tight radius |
| Budget | Start small, see 10.6 |

**Why a lead form and not a website link:** the form opens inside Meta and
pre-fills their details. Two taps instead of loading your site and typing on
a phone. Cost per lead is usually significantly lower.

The trade-off is real: lead form leads are lower intent because they are
easier to submit. That is exactly why the appointment agent matters. It
answers in under a minute and qualifies them. Without it, lead form leads are
often worthless. With it, they are the cheapest members you will get.

### The offer decides everything

More than targeting, more than creative.

| Offer | Works? |
|-------|--------|
| "Free week trial" | Yes. Simple, low friction |
| "Six week challenge, $99" | Yes. Paid means committed, and it filters |
| "Two free kids classes" | Yes. Parents respond well |
| "First month half price" | Weaker. Feels like a discount, not an event |
| "Join our gym" | No. Not an offer |
| "Book a free consultation" | No. Sounds like work |

A paid challenge produces fewer, better leads. A free trial produces more,
softer ones. Both work. Free trial suits gyms with capacity to fill. Paid
challenge suits gyms who are picky.

## Campaign 2: Retargeting

Shows ads to people who already interacted: visited your site, watched a
video, engaged with your page, or are on your list.

| | |
|---|---|
| Objective | Leads, or traffic |
| Audience | Website visitors, video viewers, page engagers, your customer list |
| Budget | Small. The audience is small |

Cheap, and it converts better than cold traffic because they already know
you. Turn it on once campaign 1 has been running long enough to build an
audience.

**The best retargeting audience for a gym:** people who watched more than 50%
of a video of an actual class. They have seen what it looks like and they are
still watching.

## Campaign 3: Local awareness

| | |
|---|---|
| Objective | Reach or engagement |
| Audience | Tight local radius |
| Budget | Small, always on |

Not a lead campaign. It keeps your gym in front of people locally so that
when they do decide, you are the name they already know.

**Do not run this until 1 and 2 are working.** It is the hardest to measure
and the easiest to waste money on. Plenty of gyms run only this, get no
leads, and conclude ads do not work.

## The order

| Month | Run |
|-------|-----|
| 1 | Campaign 1 only. Learn your cost per lead |
| 2 | Add campaign 2, once there is an audience |
| 3+ | Add campaign 3, if 1 and 2 are profitable |

## Targeting for a gym

**Radius.** Tighter than you think. People do not drive far to a gym. Three to
five miles in a city, up to ten rurally. A wide radius produces cheap leads
who never turn up.

**Age and interests.** Start broad. Meta's targeting is better than yours
when you give it a clear objective and enough conversions. Do not stack six
interest filters on day one.

**Exclude your existing members.** Upload your customer list as an exclusion.
Otherwise you pay to advertise to people who already pay you, and they notice.

## Creative that works for gyms

Ranked by what actually performs:

1. **Real footage of real classes.** Phone video is fine. Better than fine.
2. **A coach talking to camera** about who the offer is for.
3. **Before and after**, with genuine permission and no absurd claims.
4. Stock gym photos. Weakest by a distance. Everybody uses them and they
   signal that you had nothing real to show.

Film one class this week on your phone. That footage will outperform anything
you can buy.

## Video script

**Hook.** Three campaigns work for gyms. Most gym owners run a fourth thing
that does not, then decide ads do not work.

**Beats.**
1. On screen: campaign 1. Lead form. Explain the trade-off honestly: lower
   intent, which is exactly why the agent matters.
2. On screen: the offer table. Read the weak ones out loud, they are funny and
   familiar.
3. On screen: campaigns 2 and 3, briefly. Be firm that 3 comes last.
4. On screen: targeting. Radius tighter than you think, exclude your members.
5. On screen: creative. Show real class footage next to stock. No commentary
   needed.

**Go do.** Film one class on your phone this week. That is your creative.
