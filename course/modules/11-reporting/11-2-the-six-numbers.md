# 11.2 The six numbers

**Module:** 11 Reporting and Attribution
**Video:** ~8 min
**Needs first:** 11.1
**You finish with:** the only six numbers a gym owner needs weekly

## Why this matters

You could track sixty things. Six will tell you everything that matters, and
six is a number you will actually look at.

## The six

### 1. New leads

How many new people entered the system this week, from all sources.

**Watch for:** a drop with no explanation. That usually means something
broke, not that demand fell. Check your forms and your ads before you worry.

### 2. Booked rate

Of the leads that came in, what percentage got onto the calendar.

`Bookings / New leads`

**Target: 40 to 60%.** Below 40% and something is wrong with the agent or the
calendar. See 09.4 and 03.4.

**This is the number your agents most directly control.** If it moves after
you edit an agent, the edit caused it.

### 3. Show rate

Of the people booked, what percentage turned up.

`Showed / Booked`

**Target: 50 to 70%.**

Below 50% and it is almost always your confirmation and reminders. See 09.4
and 03.6.

Only meaningful if attendance is actually being marked. See 04.3. If nobody
marks it, this number is fiction and you should ignore it rather than trust
it.

### 4. Join rate

Of the people who showed up, what percentage joined.

`Joined / Showed`

**Target: 40 to 60%.**

**This one is not about software.** This is your in-person sales process,
your facility, and how the trial actually went. No agent can fix it.

If this is low and everything upstream is healthy, spending more on ads makes
things worse, not better.

### 5. Revenue won

Value of opportunities moved to Joined this week.

Needs opportunity values set. See 04.4.

### 6. Escalation rate

What percentage of agent conversations ended up needing a human.

**Target: steady and low, not zero.**

Rising means your knowledge base is falling behind. Falling to zero can mean
agents are handling things they should escalate, which is worse than a few
escalations. See 09.13.

## The funnel, read together

The six only mean something together.

```
   New leads          100
        |  booked rate 50%
   Booked              50
        |  show rate 60%
   Showed              30
        |  join rate 50%
   Joined              15
```

Fifteen members from a hundred leads. Now you can see where the leak is.

**Find your weakest rate and fix that.** Improving a healthy step is much
harder work than fixing a broken one, and everybody's instinct is to go and
get more leads at the top, which is the most expensive fix available.

[SHOT 11.2-02]

## What to do with each

| Number low | Fix |
|-----------|-----|
| New leads | Ads, module 10. Or something broke, check first |
| Booked rate | Calendar availability 03.4, then agent 09.4 |
| Show rate | Confirmation content 09.4, reminders 03.6 |
| Join rate | Your in-person process. Not in this course |
| Revenue won | Opportunity values 04.4, or genuinely low joins |
| Escalation rate rising | Knowledge base 08.5 |

## The weekly read, five minutes

1. Open the Monday dashboard.
2. Read the six.
3. Compare each to last week.
4. Find the one that moved most.
5. If it moved down, look at the fix for that one.
6. Do one thing about it. Not four.

Doing one thing a week and measuring it beats doing five things and knowing
nothing.

## Write them down

A simple sheet, six columns, one row a week.

After three months you have a trend, and a trend tells you far more than any
single week. A bad week is noise. Three bad weeks is a problem.

[SHOT 11.2-03]

## Checklist

- [ ] All six on the Monday dashboard
- [ ] I know my target range for each
- [ ] I know which are software and which are me
- [ ] I record them weekly
- [ ] I fix the weakest rate, not the top of the funnel
- [ ] One change a week

## When it goes wrong

**Show rate is 100%.** Nobody is marking no-shows. See 04.3.

**Join rate is 90%.** Somebody is moving cards to Joined without payment.
See 04.2.

**Booked rate above 80%.** Suspicious. Check whether bookings are being
created for people who did not actually book.

**All six look great and the gym feels quiet.** Check whether cards are being
maintained at all. Numbers that all look perfect usually mean nobody is
updating anything.

## Video script

**Hook.** Six numbers. Five minutes a week. Everything else on that dashboard
is decoration.

**Beats.**
1. On screen: each of the six, in turn, with its target range. Steady, do not
   rush, these are the numbers they will live with.
2. On screen: the funnel with real numbers. A hundred leads becoming fifteen
   members. Trace it out loud.
3. On screen: talk to camera. Find the weakest rate. Say clearly that
   everybody's instinct is to buy more leads and that is the most expensive
   fix on the list.
4. On screen: join rate. Be blunt. No agent fixes this one.
5. On screen: the tracking sheet with three months of history. A bad week is
   noise.

**Go do.** Write down your six numbers for last week. Then find the weakest
rate and do one thing about it.
