# 11.4 Revenue by source, not lead count

**Module:** 11 Reporting and Attribution
**Video:** ~6 min
**Needs first:** 11.3, 04.4
**You finish with:** knowing which channel produces money, not just names

## Why this matters

Lead count is the most misleading number in marketing.

A channel producing forty leads a month looks better than one producing
twelve. Then you look at what joined, and the twelve produced six members and
the forty produced three.

Everybody optimises for the forty, because that is the number on the
dashboard.

## The widget to add

Lead source attribution by **revenue**, not by contact volume.

1. Open your Monday dashboard.
2. Add the lead source attribution widget.
3. Set it to show pipeline value or revenue closed, not lead count.

   [SHOT 11.4-01]

That one setting is the whole lesson.

## What you will probably find

Consistently, across gyms:

| Source | Volume | Quality |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Referrals | Low | Highest by a distance |
| Google search | Medium | High. They were looking for you |
| Walk-ins | Low | High |
| Meta lead ads | High | Lower per lead, decent in aggregate |
| Facebook and Instagram organic | Medium | Mixed |
| Bought or scraped lists | High | Worthless. Do not |

Referrals convert better than everything else and almost no gym has a
deliberate referral system. That gap is usually the biggest opportunity on
this dashboard.

## Cost per member by source

The number that decides where money goes.

For each source: what did it cost, and how many members did it produce.

| Source | Spend | Members | Cost per member |
|--------|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Meta ads | $600 | 6 | $100 |
| Google ads | $400 | 3 | $133 |
| Referral rewards | $150 | 5 | $30 |
| Organic social | Time | 2 | Time only |

Against a lifetime value of $1,341 from 10.6, all of those work. But the
referral programme is producing members at a third of the cost of ads.

**That table is the argument for building a referral programme**, and most
gyms never build one because they never made this table.

## Do not kill a channel on volume alone

A source producing four leads a month that all join is worth more than one
producing forty that produce three.

Read the revenue column before the lead column. Every time.

## Building the referral programme this reveals

Beyond the scope of the module, but the obvious next step, and it is simple:

1. A `source-referral` tag, already in your dictionary. See 02.3.
2. Ask, at the point of maximum happiness, which is the same moment the review
   agent fires. See 09.7.
3. A reward that is worth having and does not devalue your membership. A free
   month is common. Merch works. Cash is awkward.
4. A workflow that tracks and pays it.

Ask us to wire it. It is a small job and this dashboard will show you what it
is worth within two months.

## When it goes wrong

**Everything shows as Direct.** See 11.3.

**Revenue by source is empty.** Opportunity values are not set. See 04.4.

**A source shows revenue but you cannot think what it is.** Look at the actual
contacts in it. Usually a tagged link you forgot about, or a partner posting
about you.

## Video script

**Hook.** Two channels. One gives you forty leads, one gives you twelve.
Which do you spend more on? Wrong question, and it is the one every gym owner
answers wrongly.

**Beats.**
1. On screen: the widget by lead count, then by revenue. Same data, different
   ranking. Show them next to each other. That single comparison is the
   lesson.
2. On screen: change the metric setting on camera. One click.
3. On screen: the cost per member table. Build it live with real numbers.
4. On screen: talk to camera. Referrals. Point at the number and say most gyms
   never build a referral programme because they never made this table.

**Go do.** Change your widget to revenue. Look at the ranking. It will
probably surprise you.
