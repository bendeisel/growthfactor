# 11.1 The reporting dashboard

**Module:** 11 Reporting and Attribution
**Video:** ~7 min
**Needs first:** module 04
**You finish with:** one dashboard built for a gym owner, not a marketer

## Why this matters

The default dashboard shows you everything, which means it shows you nothing.
Forty widgets, most irrelevant to a gym.

Twenty minutes building one dashboard with six things on it, and you have a
Monday morning report you will actually read.

## The rebuild

The reporting dashboards were rebuilt substantially through 2026. Drag and
drop widgets, PDF export, and custom dashboards are now standard rather than
a higher tier feature.

If you have seen older tutorials showing a fixed dashboard, that is out of
date.

1. Open **Reporting** and create a new dashboard.

3. Add the six widgets from 11.2, one at a time. Drag them into a sensible

4. Set the default date range to **last 30 days**.

5. Save.

## Build one dashboard, not four

The temptation is a dashboard per topic: ads, sales, retention, agents.

Do not. You will check one of them and forget the others exist. One dashboard
with the six numbers that matter, checked weekly, beats four comprehensive
ones checked never.

If you genuinely need a second, make it a deep-dive you open only when the
Monday dashboard says something is wrong.

## The AI summary widget

There is a widget that generates a plain-language summary of your key metrics
for the week.

Add it, at the top. It is genuinely useful as a first read, and it will
sometimes point at something you would have scrolled past.

Do not treat it as the whole report. It summarises what happened. It does not
know that you were closed for a week, or that January is always like this.

## Date ranges, and comparing honestly

| Range | Use for |
|-------|---------|
| Last 7 days | Weekly check |
| Last 30 days | The main view |
| This month vs last month | Trend |
| This month vs same month last year | Real trend, for seasonal gyms |

**Month on month is misleading for gyms.** February will beat December for
reasons that have nothing to do with your marketing. Compare to the same
month last year once you have the history. See 10.5.

## PDF export

Useful for two things: sending a monthly summary to a business partner, and
keeping a record so you can look back a year from now and see what changed.

Export the Monday dashboard at the end of each month and drop it in a folder.
Five seconds, and in a year it is the only honest record of your growth you
will have.

## When it goes wrong

**Numbers look wrong.** Nine times out of ten it is the pipeline, not the
report. Go to module 04 and check stages are being maintained and values are
set.

**A widget shows zero.** Either genuinely zero, or the underlying data is not
being captured. Check the pipeline stage or tag it depends on.

**Dashboard is slow.** Too many widgets, or too long a date range. Six
widgets, 30 days.
