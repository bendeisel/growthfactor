## What is still on you

Three things, and the agents do not work properly until they are done.

1. **A2P registration.** Legal permission to send texts. Nothing sends before
   this clears. See 01.2. Do it today, it takes days to approve.
2. **Email domain authentication.** See 01.3.
3. **The knowledge base.** We filled in the structure. Only you know your

## What it costs

Your monthly to us covers the sub-account and what we manage. On top of that
there is usage, and usage is the part people get surprised by, so here it is
plainly.

| Thing | Billed how |
|-------|-----------|
| SMS and calls | Per message, per minute. Cents |
| Emails | Per email. Fractions of a cent |
| Conversation AI responses | Per response |
| Voice AI | Per minute, and it is the expensive one |

The wallet is where this comes out of. Set an auto recharge and a threshold on
day one, because an empty wallet means your agents stop talking mid
conversation and you will not notice until somebody tells you. See 01.6.

Voice AI pricing specifically gets its own treatment in 07.5, and HighLevel
changed it in May 2026, so trust the in-app pricing page over anything written
anywhere else, this course included.

## What you can break

Honest answer: quite a lot, and that is fine, everything is recoverable except
sending a bad message to your whole list.

Safe to change freely: knowledge base content, calendar availability, message
wording, workflow timing, pipeline stage names.

Ask us first: renaming tags in the dictionary, deleting workflows, changing
custom field names, republishing an agent you have heavily rewritten. All of
these can break the wiring between agents.

Never without a plan: bulk messaging your whole contact list. See 02.5.
