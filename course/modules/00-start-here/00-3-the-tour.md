## The four places you will actually live

Ignore the rest at first.

1. **Conversations.** Every morning. This is where you see what the agents
   said overnight and step in where you need to.
2. **Opportunities.** Every morning, 60 seconds. See 04.5.
3. **Calendars.** Who is coming in.
4. **Reporting.** Once a week. See 11.2.

## Conversations, in more detail

This is the screen that matters most, so know it properly.

- Every channel lands in one thread per person. A text, a Facebook message and
  an email from the same person are one conversation, not three.
- The filter for unread and for **assigned to me** is how you stop drowning.
- When an agent escalates, the contact gets `staff-handling` and lands in your
  queue. That tag is also what stops the agent talking while you are.
- **When you reply manually, the agent steps back.** That is the handover rule
  and it is covered properly in 09.11.

## The mobile app

Get it on day one. LeadConnector, or our white labelled version, we will send
the exact one.

What it is genuinely good for: replying to conversations from the mat,
checking who is booked today, taking a payment at the desk with Tap to Pay,
and getting notified when an agent escalates something.

What to do on the desktop instead: anything structural. Do not try to edit a
workflow or an agent on a phone.

## Contacts versus Conversations

People mix these up constantly.

- **Contacts** is the database. Who they are, their tags, their fields, their
  history. Go here to find someone, or to build a list.
- **Conversations** is the inbox. Go here to talk to someone.

Same people, two views.
