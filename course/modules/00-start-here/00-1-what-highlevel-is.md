# 00.1 What HighLevel actually is

**Module:** 00 Start Here
**Video:** ~6 min
**Needs first:** nothing
**You finish with:** a clear idea of what you just stopped paying for

## Why this matters

You are about to be handed one login that replaces most of the software you
currently pay for. If you do not understand what it swallowed, you will keep
paying for both, and you will keep working in the old tools out of habit.

## What HighLevel is

It is the system that holds every person who has ever contacted your gym, and
everything that happens to them afterwards.

That is the whole idea. A lead comes in from an ad, a form, a phone call, or
somebody walking through the door. HighLevel catches them, talks to them,
books them, reminds them, takes their money, and asks them for a review. All
in one place, with one record per person.

## What it replaces

| You probably pay for | HighLevel does it |
|----------------------|-------------------|
| A booking app | Calendars, with reminders and no-show handling |
| A texting service | Two way SMS from a real local number |
| An email tool | Email sending, with your domain authenticated |
| A CRM, or a spreadsheet | Contacts, tags, smart lists |
| A review tool | Review requests and reputation |
| A funnel or landing page builder | Funnels, forms, surveys |
| A phone system | LC Phone, call tracking, call recording |
| A membership or course platform | Memberships, courses, communities |
| Half a dozen automations held together with Zapier | Workflows |

Add those up at retail. That number is why gyms move.

## What it is not

It is not a gym check-in system. It does not scan people into class, it does
not manage your mat capacity in real time, and it is not a replacement for
Zen Planner or Kicksite if you use one for attendance. Plenty of gyms run
HighLevel for everything before and around the membership, and keep their
check-in software for what happens on the mat. That is a completely reasonable
setup and we will cover the join in 02.5.

It is also not magic. It is a very good set of tools that does nothing until
somebody wires it up. That is what we already did for you, and what the rest
of this course teaches you to change.

## The word "sub-account"

You will see it constantly.

- **Agency** is the top level. That is us, Growth Factor.
- **Sub-account** is one business inside the agency. That is you.

Your gym is a sub-account under our agency. That is how you get the agents,
the snapshot, and the settings we manage on your behalf without you having to
build any of it. When a support article says "at the agency level", that means
us, and it means come and ask rather than go looking for a menu you do not
have.
