# 03.5 Rooms and equipment

**Module:** 03 Calendars
**Video:** ~5 min
**Needs first:** 03.2
**You finish with:** bookings that cannot double book your mat space

## Why this matters

Two coaches, two intros, one mat. Both bookings look fine on their own
calendars. Both people turn up at 6pm and one of them stands around watching.

Rooms and equipment solve that. You attach a physical resource to a calendar,
and once it is booked, it is booked, regardless of which coach is free.

Only relevant if you have limited space, which most gyms do.

## When you need this

| You need it if | You can skip it if |
|----------------|-------------------|
| One mat area and several coaches | One coach doing all intros |
| A private room used for PT, massage, assessments | Everything happens on one open floor with room to spare |
| Limited gear: a ring, a cage, a lifting platform | |

## Steps

1. Open **Settings > In App Preferences** and turn on **Rooms and Equipments**.

   [SHOT 03.5-01]

2. A **Rooms** tab appears in your calendar settings area.

3. Create your rooms and equipment. Be literal about what actually constrains
   you.

   | Example | Type |
   |---------|------|
   | `Main Mat` | Room |
   | `Private Room` | Room |
   | `Ring` | Equipment |
   | `Lifting Platform` | Equipment |

   [SHOT 03.5-02]

4. Set the quantity where you have more than one. Four heavy bags is quantity
   4, and four bookings can use them before it blocks.

5. Open the calendar that needs the resource, find the **Rooms** tab, and
   associate the room or equipment with it.

   [SHOT 03.5-03]

6. Save. Now a booking on that calendar also books the room, and the room
   cannot be double booked from any other calendar.

## Keep it simple

The temptation is to model your whole gym. Do not. Every resource you add is
another thing that can block a booking you actually wanted.

Add resources only where a genuine clash has happened or obviously will. For
most gyms that is one or two: the private room and the main mat.

## The interaction with round robin

Worth understanding, because it surprises people.

Round robin finds a free coach. Rooms finds free space. A slot needs both. So
if you have five coaches and one private room, your PT calendar effectively
has the availability of one room, not five coaches.

That is correct, and it is the whole point. But it means adding a room can
make your availability look like it collapsed. It did not collapse, it got
honest.

## Test it

1. Book an intro at 6pm on a calendar attached to `Main Mat`.
2. Open a different calendar also attached to `Main Mat` and try to book 6pm.
3. It should not be offered.
4. Cancel the first booking and confirm 6pm reopens on both.

## Checklist

- [ ] Rooms and Equipments turned on, if I need it
- [ ] Only genuinely constrained resources created
- [ ] Quantities set where I have more than one
- [ ] Resources attached to the right calendars
- [ ] Tested a clash and confirmed it blocks

## When it goes wrong

**Availability dropped off a cliff after turning this on.** Expected. A
resource with quantity 1 caps everything attached to it. Check quantities, and
check you have not attached a room to a calendar that does not need it.

**The clash still happens.** The second calendar is not attached to the same
resource. Both calendars must reference the same room for it to block.

**The Rooms tab is not there.** The preference in step 1 is off, or you are
looking at a calendar type that does not support it.
