## Why this matters

Filling a knowledge base from a blank page is hard. Answering thirty specific
questions is easy.

That is the whole trick. Answer the questions, then paste the answers into the
template sections.

Full list: `assets/kb-intake-30-questions.md`.

## How to use it

Two ways.

**As a form.** We can send it as a HighLevel form so answers land on a record.
Takes most owners about 25 minutes.

**As a conversation.** Some owners would rather talk it through, and the
answers are usually better that way because you can push back on the vague
ones. Ask us to book a call.

## The five that matter most

If you answer nothing else, answer these.

### Question 5: Who is your gym not for? Be blunt.

Filters wrong-fit leads before they waste a trial slot. Owners resist it.
Write it anyway.

### Question 12: Is the agent allowed to say prices?

All of them, the starting price only, or none. This single answer changes how
half your conversations go. Decide it deliberately rather than by default.

### Question 16: Who does an escalation go to, and how are they notified?

If this is not answered, escalations go nowhere and people wait. This is the
most operationally important question on the form.

### Question 28: How should the agent describe itself if asked whether it is a
robot?

Give the exact sentence. Do not leave the agent to improvise, and do not have
it claim to be human.

### Question 30: The five questions you are sickest of answering.

The single most valuable answer on the form. Those five become your first five
FAQs, and they are the questions real people actually ask, which is worth more
than any list we could invent.

## Answering well

**Write like you talk.** Answers get used almost verbatim. If you write
corporate, the agent sounds corporate.

**Specific beats complete.** "Round the back behind the barber shop, park in
the rear lot, they ticket on the street" is worth more than three paragraphs
about your facility.

**A blank is a rule, not a gap.** Anything you leave blank becomes something
the agent escalates on, rather than something it guesses about. So leaving
something blank is a valid answer, and better than a vague one.

## After it comes back

1. Fill the template from the answers.
2. Search for `[` and confirm none survived.
3. Build the FAQ source from question 30 first.
4. Test with the ten question test from 08.2.
