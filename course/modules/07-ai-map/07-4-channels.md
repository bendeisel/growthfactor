# 07.4 Channels

**Module:** 07 The AI Map
**Video:** ~5 min
**Needs first:** 07.1
**You finish with:** the right agents on the right channels, and nothing
answering somewhere you did not expect

## Why this matters

Your agents can work across SMS, email, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, the web
chat widget, and live chat.

Being on a channel you did not intend is how a gym discovers their agent has
been answering Instagram DMs in a voice they never approved.

## The channels

| Channel | Worth it for a gym | Notes |
|---------|-------------------|-------|
| **SMS** | Essential | Where most of it happens. Needs A2P, 01.2 |
| **Web chat widget** | Essential | The thing on your site. High intent |
| **Facebook Messenger** | Yes | Where a lot of local gym enquiries land |
| **Instagram DM** | Yes, if you post | Often your busiest channel and the most ignored |
| **Email** | Yes | Slower, good for the longer follow-up touches |
| **WhatsApp** | Depends on your area | Essential in some markets, irrelevant in others |
| **Live chat** | Only if you staff it | |

## Connecting a channel

1. Open **Settings > Integrations** for Facebook and Instagram, or the
   relevant connection area.
2. Connect the page or account.
3. Confirm messages appear in Conversations.

   [SHOT 07.4-02]

Connect Facebook and Instagram together, since they share a login, and
Instagram DMs are usually the bigger win for a gym.

## Turning a channel on for an agent

Each agent has per-channel control. This is the setting to be deliberate
about.

1. Open the agent.
2. Find its channel settings.
3. Enable only the channels you want it on.

   [SHOT 07.4-03]

## What we set, and why

| Agent | Channels |
|-------|----------|
| Navigator | SMS, web chat, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp if connected |
| Appointment | SMS primary, email fallback |
| Follow-up | SMS, email on the later touches |
| Reactivation | SMS, email |
| Review | SMS, email fallback |
| Voice | Voice, plus SMS for its summary |

The navigator is the only one on social channels. That is deliberate: social
messages are almost always first-contact, which is the navigator's job.

## Channel tone

Same agent, same knowledge base, but the channels are not the same.

- **SMS.** Short. Under three sentences. No formatting.
- **Instagram DM.** Even shorter. Casual. Lowercase is fine. A formal reply on
  Instagram reads as a bot instantly.
- **Email.** Can be longer, still not marketing-formatted.
- **Web chat.** Fast, short, conversational.

The agent prompts handle this, but if the tone is wrong on one channel, that
is what to adjust. See 09.9.

## Test each channel you turn on

For each connected channel:

1. Message your gym on that channel from your own account.
2. Confirm it lands in Conversations.
3. Confirm the right agent answers.
4. Confirm the tone suits the channel.

Do this per channel. A channel that is connected but not tested is a channel
where messages might be silently piling up.

## The channel nobody checks

Instagram DMs and Facebook messages sitting unanswered for weeks is extremely
common in gyms. Connecting them to your navigator is often the single highest
return change in this whole module, because the enquiries were already there
and nobody was reading them.

## Checklist

- [ ] SMS working
- [ ] Web chat widget on the site and tested
- [ ] Facebook and Instagram connected
- [ ] I have tested every connected channel by messaging from my own account
- [ ] Each agent is only on the channels I intended
- [ ] Tone checked per channel

## When it goes wrong

**Messages on a channel are not appearing.** Reconnect the integration. Social
connections expire, usually silently, and often after a password change.

**The agent answers on a channel I did not want.** Its channel settings.

**Tone is wrong on Instagram.** The prompt. See 09.9.

## Video script

**Hook.** Your Instagram DMs are probably full of people asking about
training, and nobody has read them since March.

**Beats.**
1. On screen: the channel table. Fast.
2. On screen: connect Facebook and Instagram, live.
3. On screen: an agent's channel settings. Be deliberate, only what you meant.
4. On screen: talk to camera. Channel tone. Read an SMS reply and an Instagram
   reply out loud, show the difference.
5. On screen: message the gym from a real Instagram account, watch the agent
   answer.

**Go do.** Connect Instagram, then send your gym a DM from your personal
account and see what happens.
