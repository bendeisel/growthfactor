course.

something we would do in ten minutes, and not breaking something you did not
know was load-bearing.

## Ask first

Not because you cannot, but because these have consequences that are not
obvious.

## Just tell us

Things that are ours, not yours.

- Something is broken rather than confusing
- An agent behaved differently with no change from you
- Billing questions
- A member is upset about something the system did
- You want a seventh agent, or a new tool for an existing one
- You are opening a second location. Tell us early, A2P takes days. 09.12
- Something in a lesson does not match your screen
- A check-in integration

## Before you message us

Two minutes of preparation turns a five message exchange into one.

Have ready:

1. The contact's name or ID
2. What you expected to happen
3. What actually happened
4. The relevant part of the activity log. See 06.6
5. What you already tried

Gathering that often solves it before you finish typing, which is not a trick,
it is just what structured diagnosis does.

## Where to ask

**The community first**, for anything that is not urgent or account specific.
Your question is somebody else's question, and the answer helps four gyms
instead of one.

**Direct to us** for: broken, billing, upset member, or anything involving
your specific account data.

## The two hour rule

If you have been stuck on something for two hours, stop and message us.

Nobody is impressed that you spent an afternoon on it, and two hours of your
time is worth more than the ten minutes it takes us. This is a real rule, not
politeness.

## What we watch on our side

So you know what you do not have to.

- Platform changes that affect your setup
- Agency level settings, Labs, plan tiers
- Snapshot updates and fixes
- Whether your AI plan still makes sense for your usage. 07.5

If something changes that affects you, we will tell you rather than waiting
for you to notice.
