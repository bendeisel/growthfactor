## Why this matters

You have been through fifteen modules. You will not remember which lesson
covers what in six months, and neither will the person you hire next year.

This is the index. Bookmark it.

## The five things that matter most

If somebody only ever reads five lessons, these:

1. **02.3, the tag dictionary.** The control surface for everything.
2. **08.2, the knowledge base.** What makes the agents sound like your gym.
3. **09.10, the twelve messages.** What keeps them safe.
4. **06.3, the wiring map.** How to diagnose anything.
5. **14.1, the daily ten minutes.** What makes any of it a habit.

## Handing this to a new manager

Give them, in order:

1. Module 00, all of it
2. 02.3, the tag dictionary
3. 09.2, the handover pack
4. 14.1, the daily routine
5. This index

That is about ninety minutes and it is enough for them to run the daily
routine and know when to escalate. Everything else they can look up.
