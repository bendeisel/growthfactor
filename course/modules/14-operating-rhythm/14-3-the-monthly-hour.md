# 14.3 The monthly hour

**Module:** 14 The Operating Rhythm
**Video:** ~7 min
**Needs first:** modules 08, 11, 12
**You finish with:** the maintenance that stops the system quietly rotting

## Why this matters

A system left alone for six months does not break. It drifts.

The schedule changes and the knowledge base does not. A coach leaves and is
still on the website. A price goes up and the agents quote the old one.

Nothing breaks loudly. It just gets slightly wrong, everywhere, and one day
somebody tells you your bot quoted them last year's price.

One hour a month prevents all of it.

## The routine

### 1. Read the knowledge base, twenty minutes

Top to bottom. See 08.5.

Check specifically:

- Prices
- Schedule
- Coaches, added and departed
- The current offer
- "What has changed since you left", used by the reactivation agent. See 09.6
- Anything seasonal

Then re-crawl your website, and search the base for `[` in case something new
got pasted in with a placeholder still in it.

[SHOT 14.3-01]

### 2. Read the website, ten minutes

Same drift, same fixes. Prices, schedule, coaches, offers.

**Do this straight after the knowledge base**, because if you crawl your site
into the base and the site is wrong, you have just fed it bad information.
See 08.5.

### 3. The agent scorecard, ten minutes

From 11.5. Which agent produced what, which has the highest escalation rate,
and which would benefit most from thirty minutes of your time.

Then spend the thirty minutes, this month or next. See 09.9.

### 4. The month's numbers, ten minutes

Export the Monday dashboard as a PDF and save it. See 11.1.

Compare this month to the same month last year if you have it, not to last
month. See 10.5.

### 5. Housekeeping, ten minutes

| Check | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Stale `staff-handling` tags | Contacts nobody is talking to. See 02.4 |
| Contracts expiring in 60 days | Renewal conversations. See 02.2 |
| Failed payments not resolved | Money. See 05.6 |
| Tag list, quarterly rather than monthly | See 02.4 |

The stale `staff-handling` check is the one that finds real people who were
forgotten. Do it every month.

## Quarterly, on top

Every third month, add:

- Tag hygiene clean. 02.4
- Full read of every agent prompt, checking your edits are still there. 09.9
- Re-test the twelve messages on your two busiest agents. 09.10
- Review your AI plan against your usage. 07.5
- Plan the next quarter's events. 12.3

## Yearly

- Block next year's closures in the calendar. 03.4
- Recalculate your lifetime value and cost per member. 10.6
- Review the whole course for anything the platform has changed

That last one matters more than it sounds. This platform ships changes
constantly. When something in a lesson no longer matches your screen, tell us
and we will update it for everybody.

## What triggers an unscheduled review

Do not wait for the first of the month if:

- You changed your prices
- You changed your schedule
- A coach joined or left
- You launched a new programme
- You changed your offer

Those five need the knowledge base updated the same day. See 08.5.

- [ ] Expiring contracts noted
- [ ] Quarterly and yearly items scheduled

## When it goes wrong

**An hour is not enough.** First time it will not be. Do the knowledge base
this month and the rest next month. After two or three cycles it fits.

**I keep finding the same things out of date.** Something changed and nobody
updated it at the time. Use the five triggers above and fix it on the day,
not at month end.

**Nothing needed changing.** Good. Do it again next month anyway, because the
month you skip is the month your prices go up.

## Video script

**Hook.** Nothing here breaks loudly. It drifts. Your schedule changes, your
knowledge base does not, and one day your bot quotes somebody last year's
price.

**Beats.**
1. On screen: the knowledge base read. Find something genuinely out of date on
   a real account and fix it.
2. On screen: talk to camera. Read the website before you re-crawl it. Say
   why, it is a mistake people make once.
3. On screen: the scorecard, and picking the agent to improve.
4. On screen: housekeeping. Find a stale `staff-handling` and open that
   conversation. Point out it is a real person who was forgotten.
5. On screen: the five triggers for an unscheduled review.

**Go do.** Book one hour a month, recurring. Do the knowledge base read this
month and nothing else.
