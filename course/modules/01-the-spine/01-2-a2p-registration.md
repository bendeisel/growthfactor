# 01.2 A2P 10DLC registration

**Module:** 01 The Spine
**Video:** ~10 min
**Needs first:** 01.1
**You finish with:** a submitted brand and campaign, and a realistic date for
when your texts start working

## Why this matters

In the US, businesses cannot text from a normal ten digit number without
registering. This is carrier law, not a HighLevel rule, and there is no way
around it. Unregistered traffic gets filtered or blocked outright.

Until this clears:

- The appointment agent cannot text a lead
- The follow-up agent cannot run
- The reactivation agent cannot run
- The voice receptionist cannot send its summary text
- Your reminders do not go out

Four of your six agents are dead until this is approved. **Do it today.**

## What you need in front of you before you start

Get all of this on the desk first. Half-finishing this form and coming back is
how mistakes get in.

- Your **EIN**, and the IRS letter it came on
- **Legal business name**, exactly as on that letter
- Business address, matching your registration
- A **support email** on your own domain
- Your **website URL**, with privacy policy and terms live
- A **sample message** you will actually send
- Your **opt-in flow**: where people give you permission, and a public URL
  showing it

## How it works

Two stages, in order.

1. **Brand registration.** Who you are as a business. Usually approved in
   minutes to a few hours.
2. **Campaign registration.** What you intend to send. Submitted to The
   Campaign Registry, then to each carrier. Typically one to five business
   days.

Once the brand is approved, HighLevel submits the campaign for you.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings > Phone Numbers**, then the **LC Phone** section, and find
   the **A2P Compliance** tab or the A2P registration card.

   [SHOT 01.2-01]

2. Start **Brand Registration**.

3. **Legal Company Name.** Paste it from your EIN letter. Do not type it from
   memory.

4. **EIN.** Nine digits, no dash, unless the field formats it for you.

5. Company type, industry, address, website, support email. Industry: pick
   fitness or the closest match.

6. Submit the brand. Wait. Refresh the page rather than submitting again.

   [SHOT 01.2-02]

7. When the brand is approved, complete the **campaign**. Use case: pick the
   mixed or marketing use case that matches what you actually send. A gym
   sending reminders and promotions is usually mixed.

8. **Sample messages.** Write two real ones. Not placeholders. They must
   include your business name and an opt-out instruction.

   > `Hi Sarah, this is Nashville MMA. Your free trial class is Tuesday at
   > 6pm, 123 Example Rd. Bring shorts and water. Reply STOP to opt out.`

9. **Opt-in description and proof URL.** Describe in plain words how somebody
   agrees to be texted, and give the URL where that consent appears. Usually
   your web form with the consent checkbox next to the phone field.

   [SHOT 01.2-03]

10. Submit and wait.

## The four things that get you rejected

In order of how often they happen.

1. **Legal name does not match the EIN.** Character for character. `LLC` vs
   `L.L.C.` matters.
2. **Website missing privacy policy or terms.** Reviewers open your site.
3. **No visible consent language.** There must be a checkbox or clear text
   next to the phone field on your form saying they agree to be contacted by
   text, with message rates and STOP wording.
4. **Sample messages that are placeholders.** `Hello {{name}}, thanks!` gets
   rejected. Write real ones.

## While you wait

Do not sit idle for five days. Everything in modules 02 through 08 can be
built with A2P pending. Email still works. Keep going.

## Test it, once approved

1. Send yourself a text from Conversations to your `ZZ Test` contact.
2. Reply to it. Confirm the reply lands in the thread.
3. Text `STOP` from your own phone. Confirm you get the opt-out confirmation
   and the contact gets marked. Then text `START` to undo it.

That last one matters. If STOP does not work properly you have a compliance
problem, not a software problem.

## When it goes wrong

**Rejected.** Read the reason before resubmitting. Resubmitting the same thing
costs you another cycle. Fix the specific item, then resubmit.

**Approved but messages still not delivering.** Check the number is actually
attached to the approved campaign, and check the number is assigned in the
sub-account.

**Taking longer than five days.** That is carrier queue time and nobody can
speed it up, including us. Chasing does not help.
