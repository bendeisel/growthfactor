# 01.1 Business profile

**Module:** 01 The Spine
**Video:** ~4 min
**Needs first:** nothing
**You finish with:** the legal and public details of your gym recorded once,
correctly

## Why this matters

Two reasons, one boring and one that costs money.

The boring one: this is where your address, phone and business name come from
for everything the system sends.

The one that costs money: the legal name and EIN you type here have to match
your A2P registration in 01.2 exactly. The most common reason A2P gets
rejected is a mismatch between the legal name and the EIN. Get it right here
and 01.2 goes smoothly.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings > Business Profile**.

   [SHOT 01.1-01]

2. **Legal Business Name.** Type it exactly as it appears on your EIN
   confirmation letter from the IRS. Include the entity type.
   `Nashville MMA LLC`, not `Nashville MMA`, not `Nashville M.M.A., LLC` unless
   that is literally what the letter says.

3. **Business Email.** Use a real monitored inbox on your own domain. Not a
   gmail address, and not one nobody reads.

4. **Business Phone.** The number a member should actually call.

5. **Address.** The physical gym. This ends up in message footers and on
   compliance records, so a PO box will cause problems later.

6. **Website.** Your real site. It needs to be live and it needs a privacy
   policy and terms page before 01.2, because A2P reviewers check.

7. **Time zone.** Set it to the gym's local time. Get this wrong and every
   scheduled message, every quiet hours rule and every calendar slot is off by
   hours. This is the single most common cause of "the agent texted my member
   at 3am".

   [SHOT 01.1-02]

8. Click **Save**.

## The website requirement, before you go further

A2P registration in the next lesson checks that your website has:

- A visible privacy policy
- Terms of service
- If you collect phone numbers on a form, clear consent language next to it

If your site is missing any of these, fix it now. This is the second most
common A2P rejection after the name mismatch. If your site is a mess, module
13 rebuilds it, but do not wait for that. Add the pages today, even if they
are ugly.

## Checklist

- [ ] Legal name matches the EIN letter character for character
- [ ] Business email is on my own domain and monitored
- [ ] Physical address, not a PO box
- [ ] Time zone is my local time
- [ ] My website has a privacy policy and terms page, live right now
- [ ] Consent language sits next to every phone field on my forms

## When it goes wrong

**Messages arriving at strange hours.** Time zone. Fix it here, then check
every workflow that has a wait step.

**A2P rejected for "brand mismatch".** The legal name here does not match the
EIN. Read the IRS letter again, not your bank statement.

## Video script

**Hook.** One field on this screen decides whether your text messages work at
all. Get it wrong and you wait another five days.

**Beats.**
1. On screen: Business Profile. Legal name. Hold up an EIN letter, show the
   match. This is the whole point of the lesson.
2. On screen: address, email, website. Fast.
3. On screen: time zone. The 3am text story. Ten seconds, it sticks.
4. On screen: a website footer with privacy and terms. Tell them to check
   theirs right now.

**Go do.** Open your own website and confirm the privacy policy and terms
pages exist and load. If they do not, that is tonight's job.
