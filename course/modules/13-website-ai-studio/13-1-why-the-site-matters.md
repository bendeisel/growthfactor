# 13.1 Why the site is the highest leverage thing you own

**Module:** 13 Website
**Video:** ~5 min
**Needs first:** nothing
**You finish with:** an honest assessment of your current site

## Why this matters

Every ad you run, every Google search, every person a member tells about you,
ends up looking at your website before they decide.

It is the one asset that touches every channel. It is also, in most gyms, the
oldest and worst thing they own.

## What a gym site has to do

Four jobs, in order.

1. **Convince somebody nervous that this place is for them.** Most people
   looking at a gym site are slightly intimidated. That is the emotional job.
2. **Answer the questions.** Schedule, price, location, what to bring. If they
   cannot find the schedule, they leave.
3. **Get them to enquire.** One clear action, on every page.
4. **Rank for local searches.** Programme pages do this work.

Most gym sites do the fourth badly, the second worse, and never attempt the
first.

## The honest audit

Open your current site on your phone, right now, and answer these.

| Question | Yes or no |
|----------|-----------|
| Does it load in under three seconds on mobile data? | |
| Can I find the class schedule in one tap? | |
| Is the price visible, or is it deliberately hidden? | |
| Are there photos of actual members, or stock photos? | |
| Is there a way to enquire on every page? | |
| Does it say who it is for? | |
| Would somebody nervous feel better after reading it? | |
| Is anything on it out of date? | |

Four or more noes means rebuilding is worth your time.

**Also do this:** open your site and open the two best gym sites in your city,
side by side, on your phone. That comparison is more useful than any checklist.

## The out of date problem, specifically

Whatever else you decide, go and find the out of date pages today.

An old pricing page or an old schedule does two kinds of damage. It misleads
people, and if you have crawled your site into your knowledge base, your
agents are now quoting it. See 08.5.

## What this module gives you

You can build a real gym site with Google AI Studio, for free, in a few hours,
and it can be better than most gym sites in your town.

What it will not be is as good as one we build. That is not false modesty, it
is the difference between a few hours and a few weeks, and it is why 13.6
exists.

But a site you built in an afternoon that answers the four jobs above beats
the six year old one you are embarrassed by, and it beats waiting six months
for budget.

## Should you build it or buy it

| Build it yourself if | Get it done for you if |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| Budget is tight now | The site is a real bottleneck |
| You enjoy this kind of thing | You have no time and know it |
| Your current site is genuinely awful | You want it to look like the best site in your city |
| You want to understand how it works | You want the SEO done properly |

Both are legitimate. Do not let this module make you feel obliged to spend an
afternoon on something you will hate doing.
