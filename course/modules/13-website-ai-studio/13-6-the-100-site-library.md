# 13.6 The 100 site library

**Module:** 13 Website
**Format:** Written lesson only. Not in the video track.

## The honest position

You have just built a website. If it went well, it is better than most gym
sites in your town and it cost you an afternoon.

There are two reasons people stop here anyway.

1. **You did not enjoy it**, and you will not maintain it.
2. **You want it to be the best gym site in your city**, not a good one.

Both are fair, and neither is a failure.

## What the library is

100 gym and martial arts site builds, done to the Growth Factor standard.

Every one of them is:

- Built dark and cinematic, on the standard in 13.4
- Structured with the real page set from 13.3, including a page per programme
- Running the shared class data model, so a class time changes in one place
- Wired to HighLevel out of the box: forms, chat widget, booking, tracking
- Built around your photos and your brand colours, not a template's

## When it is worth it

**Worth it if:**

- Your site is a real bottleneck and you know it
- You have programmes you should be ranking for and are not
- You tried module 13 and did not enjoy a minute of it
- You are opening a second location
- Your current site is on a platform you cannot edit

**Not worth it if:**

- You just built something you are happy with
- Your problem is that nobody visits the site, in which case module 10 is the
  answer, not a rebuild
- Your problem is that visitors do not convert, in which case check your
  offer and your agents first

That middle case matters. A better site does not fix a traffic problem, and
plenty of gyms rebuild when they should have run ads.

## What it does not include

Being straight about scope.

- Ongoing content writing
- Blog posts
- Photography. We work with your photos, and if you do not have good ones,
  that is worth fixing first and it is not expensive
- Social media
- Ads. That is module 10

## The order that works

1. Finish module 08 and 09 first. A great site pointing at agents that do not
   know your prices is a waste.
2. Then the site.
3. Then ads.

Sites before agents is the wrong order and it is the most common way gyms
spend money in the wrong sequence.

## How to start

Message us with:

We come back with a preview URL. You approve it before anything changes on
your live domain.

## If you are staying with your own build

Genuinely fine, and here is what to do to keep it good.

- Re-run the two tests in 13.4 every few months
- Keep the schedule correct. It is the most visited page
- Add a programme page whenever you add a programme
- Re-crawl the site into your knowledge base after any change. See 08.5
- Keep the photos current. A photo of a coach who left two years ago is worse
  than no photo

The site you built is yours. Look after it and it will keep working.
