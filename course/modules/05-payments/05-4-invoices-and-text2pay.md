# 05.4 Invoices, estimates and text2pay

**Module:** 05 Payments
**Video:** ~6 min
**Needs first:** 05.2
**You finish with:** a way to bill for anything that is not a standard
membership

## Why this matters

Not everything is a membership. Seminars, private lessons, gradings, a corporate
class, six weeks of PT for one person at a price you negotiated. Invoices
handle all of it.

Text2pay handles the other half of the problem, which is that people do not
open emails and they do open texts.

## Invoices

1. Open **Payments > Invoices** and create one.

   [SHOT 05.4-01]

2. Select the contact.

3. Add line items. Either products from 05.2, or a custom line for one-off
   work.

4. Set due date and any terms.

5. **Send.** You can send by email, by text, or both. Text gets paid faster.

   [SHOT 05.4-02]

### Recurring invoices

For anything billed on a schedule that is not a subscription product. A
corporate class every month, a coach renting your mat space.

1. Set the invoice to recurring.
2. Set the frequency and the end condition.
3. Turn on **auto-payment** if the customer agrees to keep a card on file.

Auto-payment on a recurring invoice captures the first payment and then runs
the schedule automatically, which turns invoicing from a monthly chore into
nothing.

[SHOT 05.4-03]

## Estimates

An estimate is a quote the customer can accept or reject. Once accepted, it
converts to an invoice.

Worth using for anything where the price varies: a block of private lessons, a
corporate booking, a birthday party.

The reason to use an estimate rather than just texting a number: you get a
record of them accepting, and the conversion to invoice is one click rather
than retyping everything.

## Text2pay

The single most useful payment feature for a gym.

Somebody is standing in front of you, or on the phone, and they want to pay.
You send them a text with a payment request. They tap it, pay, done, without
you handling a card or finding a laptop.

1. From the contact, or from an invoice, choose to send by text.
2. They get a link.
3. They pay on their phone.

[SHOT 05.4-04]

Use it for: drop-in fees when the POS is not to hand, seminar tickets, gear,
grading fees, catching up on a missed payment.

## Which to use when

| Situation | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| Standard membership | Product and subscription, 05.2 |
| Marketed offer with a landing page | Order form, 05.3 |
| "Send me something to sign up" | Payment link, 05.3 |
| One-off custom amount, needs a record | Invoice |
| Price needs agreeing first | Estimate |
| They are in front of you with a phone | Text2pay |
| They are in front of you with a card | POS, 05.5 |

## Test it

1. Send yourself an invoice for one dollar by text.
2. Pay it on your phone.
3. Confirm it marks as paid and appears on the contact record.
4. Refund it.
5. Create an estimate, accept it, and confirm it converts.

- [ ] Recurring invoices set up for anything billed on a schedule
- [ ] Auto-payment on where the customer agreed

- [ ] Estimates used for anything negotiated

## When it goes wrong

**Invoice sent but not received.** Check email authentication from 01.3, then
resend by text. Text is more reliable for this.

**Paid but still showing unpaid.** Usually a provider sync delay. Check the
transaction list before chasing the member, and never chase somebody who has
actually paid.

**Recurring invoice charged the wrong amount.** The line items changed after
the schedule was created. Cancel and recreate rather than editing a running
schedule.

## Video script

**Hook.** Somebody wants to pay you right now and you are standing on the mat
with no card machine. This is the thirty second version.

**Beats.**
1. On screen: text2pay first, because it is the one they will use most.
   Send it to a phone, pay it on camera.
2. On screen: invoices. Build one with line items, send by text.
3. On screen: recurring with auto-payment. Frame it as turning a monthly chore
   into nothing.
4. On screen: estimates. Quick, and only for gyms who negotiate.
5. On screen: the which-to-use table.

**Go do.** Send yourself a one dollar invoice by text and pay it on your
phone. Then refund it.

## Verify on screen

- Whether text2pay is a separate feature or a send option on invoices in the
  current release.
- Auto-payment availability on recurring invoices and by provider.
