# 04.5 Your 60 second morning read

**Module:** 04 Opportunities
**Video:** ~4 min
**Needs first:** 04.2, 04.3, 02.2
**You finish with:** a habit that takes a minute and catches everything

## Why this matters

You do not need a dashboard every morning. You need to know four things, and
you can get all four in about a minute.

The point of building everything in the last four modules was to make this
minute possible. The agents did the work overnight. This is you checking it.

## The four questions

Every weekday morning, in this order.

### 1. Who needs a human?

Open the **Needs a human** smart list from 02.2.

These are people an agent could not handle. Every one is a live person waiting
for a reply. Deal with them before anything else in your day.

Usually zero to three. If it is fifteen, something is wrong with your
knowledge base and you should read 09.13.

[SHOT 04.5-01]

### 2. Who is coming in today?

Open the **Booked this week** list, or today's calendar.

Look at the names. Note anything relevant: it is a kid, they mentioned a bad
knee, they were referred by a member. Tell whichever coach is on.

Thirty seconds, and it is the difference between a stranger walking in and
somebody being greeted by name with the right context.

[SHOT 04.5-02]

### 3. What is stuck?

Open the board and look for cards that have not moved.

Specifically:

- Anything in **Showed** from more than a week ago. They came in and then
  what? Somebody needs to ask them.
- Anything in **Booked** where the date has passed. Nobody marked whether they
  turned up.
- Anything in **New Lead** older than a day. That should be impossible, and if
  it is happening, an automation is broken.

[SHOT 04.5-03]

### 4. Did yesterday get marked?

Clear yesterday's attendance if you are marking it manually. See 04.3.

## That is it

Four questions, one minute. Then close it and go and coach.

Everything else, the reporting, the numbers, the campaign performance, is a
weekly job. See 11.2 and module 14.

## Do it on the phone

This works well on the mobile app, standing at the desk with a coffee. It is
designed to be a phone habit, not a desk habit, because a desk habit will not
survive a busy week.

[SHOT 04.5-04]

## What good looks like after a month

- Needs a human: usually zero, occasionally one or two.
- Never contacted: always empty.
- Nothing in New Lead older than a day.
- Nothing in Booked with a date in the past.
- Showed column moving, not accumulating.

If all five are true, your system is running properly and you can trust it.

## When it goes wrong

**It takes ten minutes, not one.** Either you are firefighting, which is fine
temporarily, or you are reading conversations you do not need to read. Trust
the agents and only open what the four questions surface.

**Needs a human is always full.** Your knowledge base has gaps. Read
`ai_escalation_reason` across those contacts and you will see the same two or
three questions repeating. Fix them in the KB. See 08.5.

**Nothing ever moves out of Showed.** Nobody is closing. That is a sales
problem, not a software problem, and it is the most valuable thing this
routine will ever show you.
