# 04.1 What a pipeline is, in gym terms

**Module:** 04 Opportunities
**Video:** ~4 min
**Needs first:** module 02
**You finish with:** understanding why you need this and not just a contact list

## Why this matters

You already have contacts. So why a second thing?

Because a contact is a person, and an opportunity is a **deal in progress**.
The same person can be a lead this year, a member next year, lapsed the year
after, and a lead again. One contact, several opportunities over time.

More practically: contacts tell you who you know. The pipeline tells you what
is about to happen and what it is worth.

## The gym version

Think of it as the whiteboard in your office, if you had one, with a magnet
for every person who might join this month.

- A card moves left to right as they get closer to joining.
- Each card carries a value: what that membership is worth.
- Cards that stop moving are the ones you go and work.

The difference from a real whiteboard is that these cards move themselves,
because your agents and workflows move them.

## Why gym owners skip this, and why that is a mistake

Most gym owners run on feel. "It's been a good month." "Leads are down."

Feel is fine until you spend money on ads. The moment you put $800 into Meta,
you need to know whether the 40 leads it produced were worth it, and you
cannot know that without tracking what happened to each one.

The pipeline is what makes module 10 and module 11 mean anything. Without it,
reporting shows you contact counts, and contact counts do not pay rent.

## Contact, opportunity, conversation

Three views of the same person, and mixing them up causes confusion in every
support conversation we have.

| Thing | Answers |
|-------|---------|
| **Contact** | Who is this person? |
| **Conversation** | What have we said to them? |
| **Opportunity** | Are they going to join, and what is it worth? |
