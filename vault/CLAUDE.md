# Vault

This is Ben Deisel's Obsidian vault, synced across five devices with Obsidian
Sync and version controlled with git. `_system/_rules.md` governs every automated
change to it. Read that file before writing anything here.

## Boundaries

- Nothing in `50-personal/` is read, linked, summarized, or referenced from a
  business session. That folder has its own `CLAUDE.md` and its own memory file.
- `_system/dashboards/` and `_system/memory/` are generated. Hand-edit them only
  to pin a fact or fix one, and mark what you pin.
- Nothing is deleted. Things move to `70-archive/<year>/`.
- Clients listed in each other's `never_link:` never appear in the same generated
  file. Check before writing any output that names two clients.

## Writing

The voice rules in `_system/memory/identity.md` apply to everything written here
and everything written from here. No em dashes. No dated agency copy.
