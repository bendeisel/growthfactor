# Offer Architecture

**The one change that matters most:** the original ladder drops price when a prospect goes quiet. Replace that with a ladder that changes **scope** based on **what they told us they need**. Never discount on silence.

---

## 1. Why the original ladder fails

Original design:

```
Stage 1: $1,500 full offer
  ↓ no bite
Stage 2: $1,000 ads only / $500 website only
  ↓ no bite
Stage 3: niche-down single-product offer
```

Three failure modes:

1. **It teaches waiting.** Any prospect who sees the sequence twice, or talks to another prospect, learns that patience is worth $500/mo. Silence becomes a negotiating tactic we invented for them.
2. **It punishes the good buyers.** The person who says yes in week one pays 50% more than the person who ignores three emails. That asymmetry leaks — via referrals, via forums, via the client who mentions their rate at a chamber lunch.
3. **The tiers don't add up to a business.** $1,000 for two managed ad platforms is thin but survivable. $500 for a WordPress build with SEO is below delivery cost unless "$500" means monthly and the build is amortized — which the original doesn't say.

## 2. The replacement: scope ladder, not discount ladder

Every tier is a **different scope at an honest price**. Movement between tiers is triggered by *stated need*, never by *elapsed time*.

### Core — **Lead Factory** · $1,500/mo · 6-month initial term

The flagship. This is what we sell 80% of the time.

**Included**
- WordPress site on the Growth Factor system — build, host, maintain, secure, backup
- On-page + technical SEO, local SEO (GBP optimization + citation cleanup), AI-search / LLM answer optimization
- Two active ad channels (Google Search + Meta, or two campaign types on one platform)
- In-Market Match audience build and monthly refresh
- Conversion tracking, call tracking, lead inbox, monthly reporting call
- Ongoing site changes: up to 2 hours/mo included

**Not included — say this out loud, every time**
- **Media spend. The client pays platforms directly, on their own card.** Minimum recommended $1,500/mo; below $1,000/mo we decline the ads half — there isn't enough budget to learn anything.
- Additional ad platforms beyond two — $500/mo each
- Content production beyond 1 page/mo, video, photography, e-commerce, custom development

### Ads Engine · $1,000/mo · needs an existing site that converts

For prospects whose site is genuinely fine and who only want demand. **Qualifying gate — all three must be true**, and we check before quoting:
- Site loads in under 3s on mobile and has a working, tracked conversion path
- They own their domain and can grant analytics/tag access
- They have ≥$1,500/mo media budget

If the gate fails, this tier is not available to them. Saying "your site will sink the ad spend, so I won't sell you ads alone" is one of the strongest trust moves available and it routinely converts to Core.

### Site & Search · $500/mo · 12-month term, $1,500 build fee

The website/SEO half, priced honestly. **Note the build fee** — this is the correction to the original $500 tier. $500/mo alone doesn't fund a build; $1,500 up front plus $500/mo does, and it amortizes over the term.

- Everything in Core's site/SEO scope
- No paid media management
- Build fee waived on 12-month prepay, or credited if they upgrade to Core within 90 days

**This tier is a *segment* offer, not a fallback.** It goes to prospects whose signal is website/WordPress intent with no ad intent. It does not get shown to someone currently considering Core.

### Add-ons (full price, never bundled as a save)

| Add-on | Price | Sell when |
|---|---|---|
| Additional ad platform | $500/mo each | They're winning on two and want a third |
| SEO Accelerator (content, digital PR, link acquisition) | $1,500–$3,000/mo | Ranking is the constraint, not conversion |
| Landing page / funnel build | $1,200 one-time | Campaign needs a dedicated destination |
| CRM & follow-up automation build | $2,500 one-time + $250/mo | They're losing leads after we deliver them — very common, very sellable |
| Creative refresh sprint | $750/quarter | Ad fatigue in reporting |

**Rule:** an add-on is never used to rescue a stalled deal. Discounting an add-on to close Core teaches the client that everything is negotiable, forever.

## 3. Concession ladder — what to give instead of price

When a deal stalls on price, spend these in order. Every one costs less than $500/mo of margin and none of them resets our price floor.

1. **Shorten the term.** 6 months → 3-month pilot at the *same* monthly rate. Costs nothing, removes the largest perceived risk.
2. **Defer the start.** Bill from the day the site goes live rather than signature. Free, feels like a month off.
3. **Sequence the scope.** Month 1 site only, ads switch on month 2. Same $1,500, less overwhelm.
4. **Waive the setup/build fee** (Site & Search tier only).
5. **Add a bonus with high perceived and low marginal cost** — competitor signal report, GBP overhaul, review-request automation.
6. **Only then**, and only with founder approval: a time-boxed rate, written in the agreement as "introductory rate for months 1–3, reverting to $1,500 in month 4." Never an open-ended discount. Log every instance in the discount register (`04-ops/18-kpi-dashboard.md`) — if it's over 20% of closed deals, our price or our proof is wrong, not our sales skill.

## 4. Risk reversal

At $1,500/mo cold from a stranger, the objection is never really price — it's *"what if this doesn't work and I've burned four months."* Answer it structurally.

**The 90-Day Lead Guarantee.**
> If we don't deliver [N] qualified leads in your first 90 days, we work for free until we do.

Three things make this safe rather than reckless:

1. **"Qualified lead" is defined in the agreement**, not left to feeling. Recommended definition: an inbound phone call over 60 seconds, a completed form, or a booked appointment, from inside the service area, for a service the client actually offers. Wrong numbers, spam, solicitations, and existing customers don't count.
2. **N is set per vertical from real data**, not optimism, and is only offered once we have ≥2 accounts of history in that vertical. Until then, offer the *make-good* without a number: "if you're not seeing lead flow at 90 days, we keep working at no charge until you do." `[ASSUMPTION]` starting values: Home services 40 / Elective health 25 / Professional services 15 per 90 days at $1,500/mo media.
3. **The remedy is continued work, not a refund.** Refund guarantees select for the worst clients and hand them a free option. A make-good keeps us in the seat and keeps the relationship alive.

**Client-side conditions**, stated plainly and without apology: minimum media spend maintained, leads responded to within one business day, and access granted within 5 days of signature. If they won't answer their phone, no targeting on earth saves them — and saying so on the sales call demonstrates exactly the competence we're selling.

## 5. Term, billing, cancellation

- **Term:** 6 months initial for Core and Ads Engine, 12 for Site & Search, month-to-month after.
- **Why a term:** SEO and ad learning phases don't produce inside 60 days. A month-to-month client who quits at day 45 gets a bad outcome and tells people about it. The term protects the result as much as the revenue — say it that way.
- **Billing:** monthly in advance, ACH or card on file, autopay required. Media spend on the client's own card, always.
- **Cancellation:** 30 days written notice after initial term.
- **What they keep on exit:** their domain, their content, their ad accounts, an export of their site. We do not hold assets hostage. It's the right thing and it removes a real objection on the call. Our proprietary audience definitions and campaign structures stay with us.

## 6. Price integrity

The single most common way an offer like this dies is quiet erosion — one exception, then another. Guardrails:

- Published price is $1,500. It appears on the site. Hiding price invites price shopping and wastes call time on people who were never going to pay it.
- No rep may discount without founder sign-off. Concessions 1–5 above are pre-approved and need no approval.
- Annual prepay discount: 10% (one month free), and this is the *only* standing discount.
- Review pricing every 6 months against close rate. **A close rate above 40% on qualified calls means the price is too low**, not that sales is great.
